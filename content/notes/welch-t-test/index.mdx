<Definition>
Welch's t-test tests whether two independent populations have equal means. It does not assume equal variances
<Cite id="welch1947" />.
</Definition>

## Hypotheses

$H_0: \mu_1 = \mu_2$. $H_1: \mu_1 \neq \mu_2$.

## Assumptions

- Observations are independent within and between groups.
- Each group mean is approximately normal. This holds for large samples by the central limit theorem.

## Test statistic

$$
t = \frac{\bar{x}_1 - \bar{x}_2}{\sqrt{s_1^2/n_1 + s_2^2/n_2}}
$$
