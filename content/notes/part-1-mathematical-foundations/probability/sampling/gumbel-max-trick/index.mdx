<Definition>
Let $\pi_1, \dots, \pi_K$ be the probabilities of a <NoteLink to="categorical-distribution">categorical
distribution</NoteLink> with logits $\ell_k = \log \pi_k$ (up to an additive constant). Draw $G_1, \dots, G_K \iid
\Gumbel(0, 1)$, each as $G_k = -\log(-\log U_k)$ with $U_k \sim \Unif(0, 1)$. Then

$$
\argmax_k \paren{\ell_k + G_k} \sim \Cat(\pi_1, \dots, \pi_K).
$$

The Gumbel-softmax, or Concrete, sample at temperature $\tau > 0$ is the vector

$$
y_k = \frac{\exp\paren{(\ell_k + G_k)/\tau}}{\sum_{j=1}^K \exp\paren{(\ell_j + G_j)/\tau}}, \qquad k = 1, \dots, K,
$$

a point on the probability simplex that tends to the one-hot argmax as $\tau \to 0$ <Cite id="jang2017" />
<Cite id="maddison2017" />.
</Definition>

## Problem

A categorical sample is a discrete choice. Its value does not change smoothly with the logits, so
<NoteLink to="gradient">gradients</NoteLink> cannot pass through it. Models that make discrete choices inside a network,
such as discrete latent codes, hard attention or architecture choices, need a way to estimate gradients with respect to
the logits.

## Mechanism

**Sampling.** The Gumbel distribution is the limiting distribution of the maximum of many independent draws. It is
stable under maxima, and this makes the argmax of Gumbel-perturbed logits land on category $k$ with probability
exactly $\pi_k$. The method needs no normalisation: the logits may be unnormalised, because adding a constant to every
logit does not change the argmax.

**Relaxation.** The argmax is a hard, one-hot choice. The softmax at temperature $\tau$ is a smooth approximation to it.
It keeps the same Gumbel noise, so the randomness is again in a parameter-free input, as in the
<NoteLink to="reparameterisation-trick">reparameterisation trick</NoteLink>. Gradients with respect to the logits
pass through the softmax.

## Why the argmax has the categorical probabilities

The noise $G = -\log(-\log U)$ is standard Gumbel by <NoteLink to="inverse-transform-sampling">inverse-transform
sampling</NoteLink>: $G \le x$ exactly when $U \le
\exp(-e^{-x})$, so $P(G \le x) = \exp(-e^{-x})$, the Gumbel cdf. The variable $X_k = \ell_k + G_k$ is Gumbel with
location $\ell_k$. Its cdf is $F_k(x) = \exp\paren{-e^{-(x - \ell_k)}} = \exp\paren{-\pi_k e^{-x}}$, and its density is
$f_k(x) = \pi_k e^{-x} \exp\paren{-\pi_k e^{-x}}$. Category $k$ wins when $X_k = x$ and every other $X_j < x$:

$$
P\paren{\argmax_j X_j = k} = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f_k(x) \prod_{j \ne k} F_j(x)\, dx
= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \pi_k e^{-x} \exp\paren{-S e^{-x}}\, dx,
$$

where $S = \sum_j \pi_j$. Substituting $t = e^{-x}$, with $dt = -e^{-x}\, dx$, gives
$\int_0^\infty \pi_k e^{-S t}\, dt = \pi_k / S$. With normalised probabilities $S = 1$ and the result is $\pi_k$. With
unnormalised logits the $\pi_k$ are proportional to the probabilities, and dividing by $S$ normalises them.

## Worked example

Take three categories with $\pi = (0.5, 0.3, 0.2)$, so $\ell = (-0.693, -1.204, -1.609)$. Suppose the uniforms are
$U = (0.2, 0.9, 0.6)$.

1. The Gumbel draws are $G = -\log(-\log U) = (-0.476, 2.250, 0.672)$.
2. The perturbed logits are $\ell + G = (-1.169, 1.046, -0.938)$.
3. The argmax is category 2.
4. The Gumbel-softmax at $\tau = 0.5$ is the softmax of $(-2.338, 2.093, -1.875)$, which is $(0.012, 0.970, 0.018)$.
   At $\tau = 5$ it is the softmax of $(-0.234, 0.209, -0.188)$, which is $(0.277, 0.432, 0.291)$.

```python
import numpy as np

rng = np.random.default_rng(0)
logits = np.log([0.5, 0.3, 0.2])
g = -np.log(-np.log(rng.uniform(size=(100_000, 3))))
print(np.bincount(np.argmax(logits + g, axis=1)) / 100_000)   # ≈ [0.5, 0.3, 0.2]
```

## Trade-offs

- **Temperature.** A low $\tau$ gives samples close to one-hot but gradients with high variance. A high $\tau$ gives
  smooth, low-variance gradients of a quantity that no longer resembles a discrete choice. Training often starts at a
  high $\tau$ and lowers it gradually.
- **Bias.** The relaxed gradient is the gradient of a different, continuous objective. It is biased for the discrete
  objective at every $\tau > 0$. The <NoteLink to="score-function-estimator">score-function estimator</NoteLink> is
  unbiased but has higher variance.
- **Straight-through variant.** The forward pass uses the one-hot argmax and the backward pass uses the gradient of
  the softmax. The forward computation is truly discrete, and the gradient is a heuristic.
- **Top-k <NoteLink to="hypergeometric-distribution">sampling without replacement</NoteLink>.** Taking the $k$ largest
  perturbed logits gives a sample of $k$ distinct categories without replacement, with the probabilities of sequential
  sampling <Cite id="maddison2014" />.

## Pitfalls

- **Extreme uniforms.** $U = 0$ or $U = 1$ makes $\log(-\log U)$ infinite. Clamp $U$ away from both ends.
- **Mean of the relaxation.** The expected Gumbel-softmax vector is not $\pi$ in general. Only the argmax, the limit
  $\tau \to 0$, has the categorical distribution.
