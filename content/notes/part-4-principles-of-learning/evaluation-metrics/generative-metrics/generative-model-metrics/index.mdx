<Definition>
**Generative model metrics** score a model that produces new samples, such as images, by comparing a set of its
samples with a set of real data. No generated image has a reference, so the comparison is between distributions. Most
metrics embed every image with a pretrained network and compare the two clouds of feature vectors.
</Definition>

## What it is for

A <Gloss name="gan">generative adversarial network</Gloss> or a <Gloss name="diffusion-model">diffusion model</Gloss>
has no test-set likelihood that is cheap and comparable across model families. Researchers still need to rank models,
tune samplers and track training. The metrics here give one number, or a pair, from a few thousand samples, and they are
the standard scores on image-generation benchmarks <Cite id="heusel2017" />. Two properties are measured: fidelity,
whether samples look real, and diversity, whether they cover the variety of the real data.

## Main ideas

**Scoring with a classifier.** The <NoteLink to="inception-score" /> uses an <Gloss name="imagenet" /> classifier's
predicted labels. It is high when each image gets a confident label and the labels vary across images. It never looks at
real data, so it cannot detect samples that are confident and varied but unlike the training set.

**Distances between feature distributions.** The <NoteLink to="frechet-inception-distance" /> fits a Gaussian to the
features of real and of generated images and reports the Fréchet distance between the two Gaussians. The
<NoteLink to="kernel-inception-distance" /> replaces the Gaussian fit by the maximum mean discrepancy with a polynomial
kernel. KID has an unbiased estimator, while FID is biased upwards for small sample sizes.

**Fidelity and diversity as two numbers.** A single distance cannot say whether a generator fails by producing poor
samples or by missing parts of the data. <NoteLink to="precision-and-recall-for-generative-models" /> estimates the
real and generated data manifolds with $k$-nearest-neighbour balls in feature space. Precision is the fraction of
generated samples on the real manifold, and recall the fraction of real samples on the generated one
<Cite id="kynkaanniemi2019" />.

**Conditional agreement.** Text-to-image models must also follow their prompt. <NoteLink to="clip-score" /> rates an
image-caption pair by the cosine similarity of their <Gloss name="clip" /> embeddings, with no reference image.

**The feature extractor sets the metric.** Every score here inherits the biases of the network that embeds the images.
Scores computed with different networks, image sizes or sample counts are not comparable.

## How the notes fit together

Start with the Inception score, then FID, which adds real data, and KID, which removes FID's Gaussian assumption.
Precision and recall split the distance into fidelity and diversity. CLIPScore is independent of the others and
measures conditional generation.
