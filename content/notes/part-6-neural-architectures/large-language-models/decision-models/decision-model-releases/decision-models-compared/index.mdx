## Architecture

All three return one typed answer per question with a probability per allowed answer, from one read of the prompt and
without generating text. <NoteLink to="nimble">Nimble</NoteLink> and <NoteLink to="tev1">Tev1</NoteLink> are
<Gloss name="lora" /> fine-tunes of Qwen3.5 that read the probabilities of single-token answer codes (see
<NoteLink to="answer-token-scoring" />). <NoteLink to="jev">Jev</NoteLink>'s internals are unpublished
<Cite id="typesafe2026launch" />.

## Training

Nimble and Tev1 differ in loss and data at once: a candidate-only loss on a small contrastive set against a
full-vocabulary loss on a large converted set (see <NoteLink to="training-decision-models" />). Neither uses
<Gloss name="rl">reinforcement learning</Gloss> or Jev's outputs <Cite id="bespoke2026nimble" />
<Cite id="together2026tev1" />. Jev uses <Gloss name="rlcd" />, whose mechanism is unpublished.

## What is distinctive

| | strength | weakness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jev | highest mean accuracy measured; cheapest per decision; long inputs; 255 options | closed; cannot be fine-tuned; method unpublished |
| Nimble | open; documented calibration fit; shared reads on Apple Silicon | 26 options on Ollama; temperature not carried by the weights |
| Tev1 | trains and serves with standard tools; small (0.8B option) | short inputs; probabilities unevaluated |

## Comparison

**Agreement with human labels.** Bespoke's public suite draws 3,880 records from 13 human-labelled datasets
<Cite id="ollama2026nimble" />.

| model | mean accuracy | run by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jev 1.13 | 76.0% | Bespoke, through TypeSafe's API |
| Nimble 9B | 75.7% | Ollama |
| Tev1 4B | 73.3% | Ollama |
| Tev1 0.8B | 63.5% | Ollama |

Jev led on choice and yes-or-no questions and Nimble on score questions, where accuracy counts only
<Gloss name="exact-match">exact matches</Gloss> with the human level.

**Independent classification.** AIMultiple scored 19 <NoteLink to="decision-models">decision models</NoteLink> on four
tasks built from records published in July to September 2026 <Cite id="aimultiple2026decision" />. The three models' 95%
intervals overlapped on two of the four. On paper topics a <Gloss name="tf-idf" />
<NoteLink to="logistic-regression">logistic regression</NoteLink> trained on 28,595 labelled papers scored 70.4%
balanced accuracy, and no decision model scored significantly higher.

**Multi-step tasks.** On 50 browser tasks two text models completed 47 and 42, the best decision model 20, and Jev 12
to 20 across six runs <Cite id="aimultiple2026decision" />. Nimble and Tev1 could not start 24 tasks because Ollama
caps a question at 26 options.

**Latency and cost.** Bespoke timed a median 106 ms per decision for Nimble on an H100 and 247 ms for Jev through its
API <Cite id="bespoke2026nimble" />. AIMultiple found Jev cheapest per decision: USD 0.039 per 1,000 decisions,
against about nine times that for Nimble and USD 0.34 for Tev1 4B, both self-hosted on a rented A100
<Cite id="aimultiple2026decision" />.

**What cannot be concluded.** The models differ in size, loss and data together, so no result isolates the loss or
the data. TypeSafe's headline speed and cost ratios come from its own workflow evaluation, and Bespoke's guide notes
that TypeSafe has published no result on a public dataset <Cite id="bespoke2026nimble" />.
