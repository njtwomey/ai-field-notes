<Definition>
  In a <NoteLink to="pre-norm-and-post-norm">pre-norm transformer</NoteLink> the vector $\xvec_\ell \in \reals^d$ of a
  token after $\ell$ sublayers is the token's embedding plus the sum of every earlier sublayer's output at that
  position. The **residual stream view** treats this vector as a shared channel: each attention head and each
  feed-forward layer **reads** it through a linear projection (after a normalisation) and **writes** to it by adding a
  linear projection of its result <Cite id="elhage2021" />. The output head reads the final stream.
</Definition>

## Intuition

Nothing in the stream is overwritten by default. A component that writes a vector into some subspace leaves it there
for every later component to read, until another component writes the negative of it. Components communicate through
subspaces of the stream, and the analysis of a model becomes the analysis of which component writes into the subspace
that another reads.

## Details

Rows are tokens throughout: $\Xmat \in \reals^{n \times d}$ holds the stream of $n$ tokens.

### Linearity of the stream

Unrolling $\xvec_{\ell+1} = \xvec_\ell + F_\ell(\xvec_\ell)$ gives

$$
\xvec_L = \xvec_0 + \sum_{\ell=0}^{L-1} F_\ell(\xvec_\ell).
$$

Suppose a component writes $\avec\Wmat_{\text{out}}$, with $\Wmat_{\text{out}} \in \reals^{k \times d}$, and a later
component reads $\xvec\Wmat_{\text{in}}$, with $\Wmat_{\text{in}} \in \reals^{d \times m}$. The part of the later input
that comes from the earlier output is $\avec\,(\Wmat_{\text{out}}\Wmat_{\text{in}})$. The product
$\Wmat_{\text{out}}\Wmat_{\text{in}}$ is a **virtual weight** connecting the two components directly, even when many
layers separate them.

### No privileged basis

Let $\Rmat$ be any orthogonal $d \times d$ matrix. Replace every write matrix $\Wmat_{\text{out}}$ by
$\Wmat_{\text{out}}\Rmat$, the embedding $\Wmat_E$ by $\Wmat_E\Rmat$, and every read matrix $\Wmat_{\text{in}}$ by
$\Rmat^\tr\Wmat_{\text{in}}$. The stream becomes $\xvec\Rmat$, and every read gives
$\xvec\Rmat\Rmat^\tr\Wmat_{\text{in}} = \xvec\Wmat_{\text{in}}$, unchanged. The function computed is the same, so the
coordinates of the stream have no special meaning <Cite id="elhage2021" />. <Gloss name="rmsnorm" /> with unit gains
commutes with $\Rmat$, since it divides by a norm that $\Rmat$ preserves; learned gains break the symmetry only until
they are folded into the next read matrix.

### An attention head as two matrices

A head with query, key and value projections $\Wmat_Q, \Wmat_K, \Wmat_V \in \reals^{d \times d_h}$ and output rows
$\Wmat_O \in \reals^{d_h \times d}$ computes

$$
\Amat = \operatorname{softmax}\paren{\frac{\Xmat\Wmat_Q\Wmat_K^\tr\Xmat^\tr}{\sqrt{d_h}} + \Mmat}, \qquad
\operatorname{head}(\Xmat) = \Amat\,\Xmat\,\Wmat_V\Wmat_O,
$$

with the <NoteLink to="causal-masking">causal mask</NoteLink> $\Mmat$. The four projections appear only in two products:

- the **QK matrix** $\Wmat_{QK} = \Wmat_Q\Wmat_K^\tr \in \reals^{d \times d}$ decides which positions attend to which;
- the **OV matrix** $\Wmat_{OV} = \Wmat_V\Wmat_O \in \reals^{d \times d}$ decides what an attended position writes into
  the stream of the attending one.

Both have rank at most $d_h$. Splitting a head into query and key, or value and output, is a factorisation choice that
does not affect the function.

### Heads are independent and additive

<Gloss name="mha">Multi-head attention</Gloss> concatenates the head outputs $\Amat^h\Xmat\Wmat_V^h$ and multiplies by $\Wmat_O \in
\reals^{h d_h \times d}$. Split $\Wmat_O$ into row blocks $\Wmat_O^h \in \reals^{d_h \times d}$, one per head. Block
multiplication gives

$$
\operatorname{concat}_h\paren{\Amat^h\Xmat\Wmat_V^h}\,\Wmat_O = \sum_h \Amat^h\Xmat\,\Wmat_V^h\Wmat_O^h = \sum_h
\Amat^h\Xmat\,\Wmat_{OV}^h .
$$

A layer of $h$ heads is $h$ separate components writing to the stream, each with its own QK and OV matrices.

### Zero layers: bigrams

With embedding $\Wmat_E \in \reals^{V \times d}$ and unembedding $\Wmat_U \in \reals^{d \times V}$, a model with no
blocks maps a one-hot token $\tvec$ to logits $\tvec\,\Wmat_E\Wmat_U$. The $V \times V$ matrix $\Wmat_E\Wmat_U$ can only
store which token tends to follow which, a bigram table <Cite id="elhage2021" />.

### One attention-only layer: skip-trigrams

Ignore normalisation, which is a scaling that can be folded into adjacent weights, and positional terms. With one layer
of heads, the logits at destination position $i$ are

$$
\zvec_i = \underbrace{\tvec_i\,\Wmat_E\Wmat_U}_{\text{direct path}} + \sum_h \sum_j A^h_{ij}\;
\tvec_j\,\underbrace{\Wmat_E\Wmat_{OV}^h\Wmat_U}_{\text{OV circuit}}, \qquad
A^h_{ij} \propto \exp\paren{\tvec_i\,\underbrace{\Wmat_E\Wmat_{QK}^h\Wmat_E^\tr}_{\text{QK circuit}}\,\tvec_j^\tr/\sqrt{d_h}} .
$$

Both circuits are $V \times V$ tables that can be read from the weights without running the model. The QK circuit gives
the attention score of each destination token for each source token. The OV circuit says how attending to a source token
changes the logit of each output token. Together they implement **skip-trigrams** "$A \dots B \to C$": a source token
$A$ earlier in the context, attended to from the current token $B$, raises the logit of $C$. Many heads in such models
copy: their OV circuit raises the logit of the attended token itself <Cite id="elhage2021" />.

### Two layers: composition

A head in the second layer reads a stream that includes the first layer's outputs. It can use them in three places:

- **Q-composition**: its $\Wmat_Q$ reads a subspace written by an earlier head;
- **K-composition**: its $\Wmat_K$ does;
- **V-composition**: its $\Wmat_V$ does.

V-composition of heads $h_1$ and $h_2$ acts as a **virtual attention head** with pattern $\Amat^{h_2}\Amat^{h_1}$ and OV
matrix $\Wmat_{OV}^{h_1}\Wmat_{OV}^{h_2}$. Q- and K-composition change the attention pattern itself, which lets a head
attend by a relation computed by another head <Cite id="elhage2021" />. An **induction head** uses K-composition with a
previous-token head to find where the current token occurred before and copy what followed it; see
<NoteLink to="in-context-learning">in-context learning</NoteLink> and <Cite id="olsson2022" />.

## Worked example

Take a vocabulary $\{A, B, C\}$, $d = 6$ and one head with $d_h = 3$. Let the embedding write token $k$ into
coordinate $k$ (coordinates 1 to 3), let the unembedding read coordinates 4 to 6, and let the head have

- a QK circuit that, after division by $\sqrt{d_h}$, is 2 for destination $C$ and source $A$ and 0 elsewhere, so
  that $C$ looks for earlier $A$;
- an OV matrix that copies coordinate $k$ to coordinate $k + 3$, so that its OV circuit is the $3 \times 3$ identity.

The direct path $\Wmat_E\Wmat_U$ is zero, because the embedding and the unembedding use disjoint subspaces. On the
sequence $A\,B\,C$, the destination $C$ scores the sources $(A, B, C)$ as $(2, 0, 0)$, with weights
$\operatorname{softmax}(2, 0, 0) = (0.787, 0.107, 0.107)$. The head writes $0.787$ into coordinate 4, $0.107$ into
coordinate 5 and $0.107$ into coordinate 6. The logits at $C$ are $(0.787, 0.107, 0.107)$: the model predicts $A$. This
is the skip-trigram "$A \dots C \to A$". Every quantity came from reading the two circuits, without running the model.

## Assumptions

- The analysis is exact for attention-only models with patterns treated as given. Feed-forward layers also read and
  write the stream, but their elementwise nonlinearity does not reduce to $V \times V$ tables.
- Normalisations are folded into adjacent weights up to a scalar per token, and biases are omitted.

## Pitfalls

- **Bandwidth.** The stream has $d$ dimensions, but the components writing to it have many more. Llama 2 7B has
  $32 \times 32$ heads writing up to 128 dimensions each and $32 \times 11{,}008$ feed-forward units, all into
  $d = 4096$. Subspaces are shared, and some components appear to erase what others wrote <Cite id="elhage2021" />.
- **The softmax is not linear.** The path expansion is linear in the OV circuits once the attention patterns are fixed.
  The patterns themselves depend nonlinearly on the stream, which is what Q- and K-composition exploit.
- **Coordinates are not features.** Because the stream has no privileged basis, a single coordinate of it need not mean
  anything; see <NoteLink to="attention-head-analysis">attention head analysis</NoteLink> for what individual heads do.
