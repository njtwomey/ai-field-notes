<Definition>
A **diffusion model** generates data by reversing a fixed noising process. The forward process adds Gaussian noise to
a data point over many steps until only noise remains. A network is trained to undo one step at a time, by predicting
the added noise or, equivalently, the score $\nabla_{\xvec} \log p_t(\xvec)$ of the noised data distribution.
Sampling starts from pure noise and applies the learned reverse steps <Cite id="ho2020" />.
</Definition>

## What it is for

Diffusion models are the leading approach for generating images, video and audio, and are used for molecules, protein
structures and robot trajectories. They train stably with a simple regression loss, unlike adversarial training, and
they cover the modes of the data well. Their cost is sampling: generation needs tens to hundreds of network passes, so
much of the field concerns faster samplers. Conditioning on text, a class or another image is added by guidance, and
running the process in a learned latent space makes high-resolution generation affordable.

## Main ideas

**Noise in closed form, denoise by regression.** In
<NoteLink to="denoising-diffusion-probabilistic-models" /> the noised sample at step $t$ has the closed form
$\xvec_t = \sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,\xvec_0 + \sqrt{1 - \bar\alpha_t}\,\epsilonvec$, so any step can be sampled directly.
Training reduces to predicting $\epsilonvec$ with squared error, a weighted form of the evidence lower bound.

**The score unifies the views.** <NoteLink to="score-based-generative-models" /> learn the score of data blurred at
many noise levels by denoising score matching, and sample by following the score from noise to data. In continuous
time the forward process is a stochastic differential equation and sampling solves its reverse-time counterpart;
diffusion models are one instance <Cite id="song2021" />.

**Deterministic paths sample faster.** <NoteLink to="denoising-diffusion-implicit-models" /> keep the same marginals
with a non-Markovian forward process, so a trained model can be sampled in tens of steps without retraining. Its
deterministic form discretises the <NoteLink to="probability-flow-ode" />, which also gives exact likelihoods and an
invertible map from noise to data. The design space of noise schedules, preconditioning and <Gloss name="ode" /> solvers
was later organised into one framework <Cite id="karras2022" />.

**Guidance trades diversity for fidelity.** <NoteLink to="diffusion-guidance" /> samples with the score of
$p(\xvec \mid \cvec)\,p(\cvec \mid \xvec)^w$ to follow a condition $\cvec$ more closely. Classifier guidance adds a
separate classifier's gradient; classifier-free guidance extrapolates between the conditional and unconditional
predictions of one network.

**Diffuse in a compressed space.** <NoteLink to="latent-diffusion" /> trains an
<NoteLink to="autoencoder">autoencoder</NoteLink> to compress images to a smaller latent grid and runs diffusion on the
latents. Sampling denoises in latent space and decodes once, and cross-attention injects conditions such as text.
