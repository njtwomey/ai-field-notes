<Definition>
The **PinSage family** is the sequence of representation models that Pinterest published between 2018 and 2025,
together with GraphSAGE, the academic method they started from. Item-side models embed pins, boards, products and
other entities from content and graph structure. User-side models turn a user's actions into one or more vectors or
into features of a ranking model. Almost every later model takes PinSage pin embeddings as input, until OmniSage
replaced them <Cite id="ying2018pinsage" /> <Cite id="badrinath2025omnisage" />.
</Definition>

## Components

Each model has its own note.

- <NoteLink to="graphsage">GraphSAGE</NoteLink>: inductive embeddings from sampled, aggregated neighbourhoods.
- <NoteLink to="pixie-random-walk">Pixie</NoteLink>: real-time recommendation by <NoteLink to="random-walk">random walks</NoteLink> on the pin–board graph.
- <NoteLink to="pinsage">PinSage</NoteLink>: GraphSAGE at web scale with random-walk neighbourhoods,
  importance pooling and curriculum hard negatives. A shorter summary is in <NoteLink to="pinsage">PinSage</NoteLink>.
- <NoteLink to="pinnersage">PinnerSage</NoteLink>: several embeddings per user from Ward clustering of action pins.
- <NoteLink to="pinnerformer">PinnerFormer</NoteLink>: one daily user embedding from a transformer trained with the
  dense all-action loss.
- <NoteLink to="itemsage">ItemSage</NoteLink>: multi-modal product embeddings compatible with image and text queries.
- <NoteLink to="transact">TransAct</NoteLink>: a real-time action transformer inside the ranker, with TransAct V2 for
  lifelong sequences.
- <NoteLink to="omnisage">OmniSage</NoteLink>: one framework for graph, content and sequence signals on a heterogeneous
  graph.
- <NoteLink to="pinfm">PinFM</NoteLink>: a pretrained foundation model of user activity, fine-tuned inside rankers.
- <NoteLink to="pinsage-family-compared">The PinSage family compared</NoteLink>: a side-by-side case study.

## Data flow

A request to a Pinterest recommendation surface passes through the stages of a
<NoteLink to="multi-stage-recommendation">multi-stage recommender</NoteLink>, and the family supplies each stage.

1. **Item embeddings, offline.** PinSage (later OmniSage) embeds every pin in a batch job. ItemSage
   embeds every product. The vectors go to a feature store and to an
   <NoteLink to="approximate-nearest-neighbour-search">approximate nearest-neighbour</NoteLink> index such as
   <NoteLink to="hnsw">HNSW</NoteLink>.
2. **User embeddings, offline.** PinnerSage clusters each user's recent action pins daily; PinnerFormer runs a
   transformer over each active user's last 256 actions daily. Both consume the pin embeddings from step 1.
3. **Candidate retrieval, online.** Pixie walks the graph from the user's recent pins. Embedding-based retrieval
   searches the index with PinnerSage medoids, PinnerFormer or ItemSage-compatible query embeddings.
4. **Ranking, online.** The ranker combines static features, the daily PinnerFormer embedding and TransAct's encoding
   of the last 100 actions with each candidate. PinFM later added a pretrained sequence module to the homefeed and related-items rankers.

## Recurring ideas

- **Random-walk importance.** Visit counts of short walks, an estimate of personalised PageRank, choose what is related:
  candidates in Pixie, neighbourhoods and pooling weights in PinSage, typed neighbourhoods in OmniSage.
- **Fixed upstream embeddings.** Later models take PinSage vectors as frozen inputs. PinnerSage keeps pin embeddings
  fixed on purpose; ItemSage learns to be compatible with frozen PinSage and SearchSage spaces.
- **<NoteLink to="two-tower-model">Sampled softmax</NoteLink>.** PinnerFormer and ItemSage train with in-batch and random negatives and a logQ correction for how
  often each item appears as a negative. OmniSage, TransAct V2's next-action loss and PinFM's pretraining also use
  sampled-softmax contrastive losses.
- **Batch and real time.** Daily batch embeddings (PinnerSage, PinnerFormer, OmniSage) are cheap and capture long-term
  interest. Real-time sequence models in the ranker (TransAct, TransAct V2, PinFM) capture intent at a higher serving
  cost. Pinterest runs both.
- **Horizon of the objective.** PinnerFormer and PinFM predict a window of future positives rather than only the next
  action, which keeps embeddings useful between updates.

## Variants

The family moved through three stages. From 2018 to 2020 it was graph-centred: random walks and graph convolutions on
the pin–board graph, with users represented through pins. From 2022 to 2023 it became sequence-centred: transformers over
user actions, trained end to end, with PinSage embeddings as inputs. From 2024 it moved to unified and pretrained
models: OmniSage merged graph, content and sequence objectives, and PinFM applied <Gloss name="language-model">language-model</Gloss> pretraining to user
activity.
