<Definition>
A topic model applies to any data that can be cast as **documents** made of discrete **words**, where each document
mixes a few recurring patterns. Applying one means choosing what counts as a word, what counts as a document, and which
assumption of <NoteLink to="latent-dirichlet-allocation">latent Dirichlet allocation</NoteLink> to keep or change. The
notes in this topic each make that choice for one kind of data: genotypes, images, ratings and sensor streams.
</Definition>

## What it is for

The applications show that LDA is a general model of grouped categorical data, not a model of language. A topic is a
distribution over tokens that recur together, and a document's topic proportions say how much of each pattern it
contains. In genetics the patterns are ancestral populations; in images they are parts of objects or scenes; in sensor
data they are activities.

## Main ideas

**The same model under another name.** The admixture model of STRUCTURE infers ancestry from genotypes
<Cite id="pritchard2000" />. Individuals are documents, allele copies are words and ancestral populations are topics.
<NoteLink to="population-structure-admixture" /> shows that its generative process is exactly LDA's. STRUCTURE was
published three years before LDA and developed independently.

**Building a vocabulary from continuous data.** Images and accelerometer signals have no natural words. Both
applications quantise: local image descriptors, or short windows of acceleration, are assigned to the nearest centre of
a <NoteLink to="k-means" /> codebook, and the centre's index becomes the word.
<NoteLink to="bag-of-visual-words-topic-models" /> finds recurring scene parts and object categories this way.

**Choosing the document.** The document sets the scale of a pattern. <NoteLink to="activity-routines-from-wearables" />
uses overlapping 30-minute windows, so topics are routines such as commuting or office work rather than single
movements. <NoteLink to="smart-home-activity-discovery" /> cuts the event stream of a home's sensors where the resident
changes room, so each segment is one activity of daily living.

**Joining topics to another model.** <NoteLink to="collaborative-topic-regression" /> ties LDA to matrix factorisation
for recommendation. An item's latent vector is its topic proportions plus an offset learnt from ratings, so a new item
with text and no ratings can still be recommended.

## How the notes fit together

- Each note needs only <NoteLink to="latent-dirichlet-allocation">latent Dirichlet allocation</NoteLink> and stands
  alone.
- The two sensor notes compare naturally: wearables use LDA unchanged, while the smart-home model gives each segment a
  single topic and models transitions between events.
- The population-genetics note is the clearest view of the correspondence between LDA and a model from another field.
