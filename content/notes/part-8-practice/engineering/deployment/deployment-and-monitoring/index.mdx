<Definition>
**Deployment and monitoring** covers the life of a model after training: replacing the current production model with
a new one without harming users, and checking continuously that the deployed model still behaves as it did when it was
evaluated.
</Definition>

## What it is for

A model's offline evaluation describes the data it was tested on. Production differs. Requests come from a population
that changes with time, upstream pipelines change the meaning or scale of features, and true labels may arrive weeks
late or never. <NoteLink to="machine-learning-engineering">Machine learning systems</NoteLink> also fail silently: a
broken feature yields confident wrong predictions rather than an error <Cite id="sculley2015" />. The notes here cover
safe rollout and the checks that catch such failures.

## Main ideas

**Release in stages.** <NoteLink to="shadow-deployment-and-canary-releases" /> describes two rollout patterns. A shadow
deployment runs the new model on copies of live requests without serving its outputs, which tests it on real traffic at
no risk to users. A canary release serves the new model to a small share $f$ of traffic and compares it with the rest.
The precision of that comparison grows as $\sqrt{f(1 - f)}$ while the exposure to a bad model grows as $f$, so canaries
stay small.

**Watch every layer of the system.** <NoteLink to="model-monitoring" /> tracks service health, input data, predictions
and, when labels arrive, accuracy, and raises alerts when any of them departs from its expected range. Some checks need
no labels. For a calibrated classifier the mean predicted probability equals the positive rate, so a gap between the
two signals a problem before labels are available.

**Test for change.** <NoteLink to="drift-detection" /> compares a window of recent inputs or predictions with a
reference sample by a two-sample test: the <Gloss name="ks-test">Kolmogorov–Smirnov test</Gloss> for one feature, the
<Gloss name="mmd">maximum mean discrepancy</Gloss> for vectors, or the population stability index over binned shares
<Cite id="gama2014" />. A detected drift calls for investigation, not automatic retraining, since some shifts do not
affect accuracy.

## How the notes fit together

Read <NoteLink to="shadow-deployment-and-canary-releases" /> first, since rollout comes before monitoring. Model
monitoring sets out what to watch. Drift detection gives the <NoteLink to="statistical-tests">statistical
tests</NoteLink> used for the input and prediction checks.
