<Definition>
An **energy-based network** is a network of units $s_i$, usually binary, joined by symmetric weights $w_{ij} = w_{ji}$,
whose joint state $\svec$ has the energy

$$
E(\svec) = -\frac12\sum_{i \neq j} w_{ij} s_i s_j - \sum_i b_i s_i = -\frac12\svec^\top\Wmat\svec - \bvec^\top\svec,
$$

with $w_{ii} = 0$. The energy is that of the <NoteLink to="ising-model">Ising model</NoteLink> of a magnet, with units
for spins, weights for couplings and biases for fields. The network either moves downhill in this energy to recall a
stored state, as a <NoteLink to="hopfield-network">Hopfield network</NoteLink> does, or samples states with
probability $p(\svec) \propto e^{-E(\svec)/T}$ and learns the weights so that this distribution fits data, as a
<NoteLink to="boltzmann-machine">Boltzmann machine</NoteLink> does.
</Definition>

## One energy, two uses

The same quadratic energy supports two kinds of computation, told apart by the temperature $T$.

**At $T = 0$: memory by descent.** Unit $k$ appears in the energy only through the term $-s_k h_k$, where $h_k =
\sum_j w_{kj}s_j + b_k$ is its local field. Setting $s_k$ to the sign of $h_k$ therefore never raises the energy, and
updating units one at a time slides the state into a local minimum. If the weights make chosen patterns the minima, a
corrupted pattern slides back to the original: an <Gloss name="associative-memory">associative memory</Gloss>.

**At $T > 0$: a probability distribution.** With $\pm1$ units, the two values of $s_k$ given the others have energies
$\mp h_k$ plus the same remainder, so under $p(\svec) \propto e^{-E(\svec)/T}$

$$
\pr(s_k = +1 \mid \text{all other units}) = \frac{e^{h_k/T}}{e^{h_k/T} + e^{-h_k/T}} = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-2h_k/T}}.
$$

Updating units with this probability is <NoteLink to="gibbs-sampling">Gibbs sampling</NoteLink> of the <Gloss name="boltzmann-distribution">Boltzmann
distribution</Gloss>. As $T \to 0$ the probability tends to $1$ for $h_k > 0$ and to $0$ for $h_k < 0$, which is the
deterministic update above: the Hopfield network is the zero-temperature limit of the stochastic network. Raising
$T$ lets the state climb out of shallow minima, and lowering it slowly is
<NoteLink to="simulated-annealing">simulated annealing</NoteLink>. Learning the weights from data, with hidden units
that the data do not set, turns the stochastic network into a generative model.

## Components



- <NoteLink to="ising-model">Ising model</NoteLink>: spins, couplings, the Boltzmann distribution, the phase
  transition and Metropolis and Glauber dynamics.
- <NoteLink to="spin-glass">Spin glass</NoteLink>: random couplings, frustration and rugged landscapes with many
  minima.
- <NoteLink to="hopfield-network">Hopfield network</NoteLink>: Hebbian storage, the proof that recall descends the
  energy, attractors, spurious states and the capacity $0.138N$.
- <NoteLink to="boltzmann-machine">Boltzmann machine</NoteLink>: stochastic units, hidden units and the learning rule
  $\langle s_is_j\rangle_{\text{data}} - \langle s_is_j\rangle_{\text{model}}$.
- <NoteLink to="restricted-boltzmann-machine">Restricted Boltzmann machine</NoteLink>: the bipartite version, its
  free energy and <Gloss name="contrastive-divergence">contrastive divergence</Gloss>.
- <NoteLink to="deep-belief-network">Deep belief network</NoteLink>: stacks of restricted Boltzmann machines trained greedily, layer by layer.
- <NoteLink to="modern-hopfield-network">Modern Hopfield network</NoteLink>: sharper energies, exponential capacity and
  the equivalence of the continuous update with attention.

## The 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences awarded the 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics to John Hopfield and Geoffrey Hinton
"for foundational discoveries and inventions that enable machine learning with artificial neural networks"
<Cite id="nobel2024" />. The committee's scientific background cites the Hopfield network of 1982 and the Boltzmann
machine of 1985 as the two contributions, and places both in statistical physics. The Hopfield network is a spin
system whose energy landscape stores memories, analysed with the tools of magnetism and spin glasses. The Boltzmann
machine takes its name and its sampling rule from Boltzmann's distribution of statistical mechanics, and learns by
matching the correlations of a physical system to those of data. The background also traces the line to restricted
Boltzmann machines, deep belief nets and the pretraining that helped start modern deep learning.

## Variants and descendants

- **Continuous and graded units.** Hopfield's 1984 network uses graded sigmoid units with the same descent property.
- **Energy-based models with neural energies.** Modern <NoteLink to="energy-based-models">energy-based
  models</NoteLink> replace the quadratic energy by any neural network $E_{\thetavec}(\xvec)$ and train it with the
  same two-phase <NoteLink to="gradient">gradient</NoteLink>, sampled by <NoteLink to="langevin-dynamics">Langevin dynamics</NoteLink>.
- **Markov random fields.** As probabilistic models, Boltzmann machines are pairwise
  <NoteLink to="markov-random-field">Markov random fields</NoteLink> over binary variables, some of them hidden.
- **Attention.** The continuous modern Hopfield update is the <NoteLink to="scaled-dot-product-attention">softmax attention</NoteLink> of transformers, so every attention
  head performs one step of associative recall.
