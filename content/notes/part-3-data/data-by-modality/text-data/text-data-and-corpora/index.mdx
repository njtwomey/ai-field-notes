<Definition>
**Text data** are sequences of characters, in some encoding and script, grouped into documents, turns or records. A
**corpus** is a collection of such documents assembled for a purpose, such as pretraining a
<Gloss name="language-model">language model</Gloss>, building a search index or fine-tuning an assistant. The chapter
covers how raw text is cleaned, how corpora are built and kept apart from benchmarks, and how text is labelled and
augmented.
</Definition>

## What it is for

Language models are trained on trillions of tokens of web text, code and books. Classifiers, taggers and retrievers are
trained on smaller labelled sets. In both cases the text is dirty when it arrives: wrong encodings, markup, boilerplate,
mixed languages, near-duplicate pages, <NoteLink to="personal-data-and-identifiability">personal data</NoteLink> and
copies of evaluation sets. Filtering and deduplication choices alone produce clear differences in the benchmark scores
of language models trained on the result <Cite id="penedo2024" />. Fine-tuning data then decide its behaviour as an
assistant <Cite id="ouyang2022" />.

## Main ideas

**Clean characters before words.** <NoteLink to="text-cleaning" /> fixes character encodings, normalises Unicode,
removes markup and boilerplate and identifies each document's language. <NoteLink to="multilingual-text" /> handles
scripts without spaces, transliteration, code-switching and the unequal number of tokens different languages need for
the same content.

**Build corpora by filtering and mixing.** <NoteLink to="corpus-construction" /> crawls or collects text, filters it for
quality and safety, removes near-duplicates and personal data, and mixes sources in chosen proportions
<Cite id="dodge2021" />. <NoteLink to="code-data" /> deals with forks, vendored libraries, generated files, licences and
secrets. <NoteLink to="retrieval-corpora" /> chunks documents into passages, builds query-passage pairs and mines hard
negatives, against relevance judgements that are always incomplete.

**Keep benchmarks out of training data.** <NoteLink to="benchmark-contamination" /> detects test items in training
corpora by n-gram overlap, embedding similarity or likelihood probes, and prevents contamination with canary strings
and date cut-offs.

**Supervision for assistants comes as conversations.** <NoteLink to="instruction-and-preference-data" /> pairs prompts
with demonstrations for supervised fine-tuning, and with ranked completions for reward modelling and direct preference
optimisation. <NoteLink to="dialogue-and-agent-traces" /> keeps the structure of multi-turn conversations and of agent
trajectories with tool calls and observations.

**Labels sit on spans of text.** <NoteLink to="span-annotation-and-token-alignment" /> encodes spans with BIO tags or
span lists, handles nested and discontinuous spans, and aligns character-level labels to subword tokens.

**Augmentation must keep the label.** <NoteLink to="text-augmentation" /> uses word and character edits,
back-translation and paraphrases from language models, and filters the results.
