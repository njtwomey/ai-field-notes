<Definition>
**Multimodal data** describe one event or object through several modalities, such as image and text, audio and video,
or sensors and annotations. **Paired data** link an example in one modality to its counterpart in another. Preparing
them means aligning the modalities in time or meaning, deciding what to do when one is missing, and arranging them so a
model can combine them.
</Definition>

## What it is for

Image-text models such as CLIP learn from hundreds of millions of image-caption pairs <Cite id="radford2021" />. Speech
recognisers learn from audio paired with transcripts, video models from clips paired with narration, and wearable
systems from several sensors paired with labels. Each pairing can be exact, loose or wrong. Streams recorded by separate
devices drift apart in time. Some examples lack a modality, and some modalities are learned faster and drown out the
rest. The choices made in preparation decide what a multimodal model can learn from each part
<Cite id="baltrusaitis2019" />.

## Main ideas

**Put streams on one clock.** <NoteLink to="temporal-synchronisation" /> estimates each device's clock offset and drift
against a reference, by cross-correlation or shared events, and maps every timestamp onto one timeline. Labels are
aligned to signals the same way.

**Pairs vary in quality and cardinality.** <NoteLink to="cross-modal-pairing" /> distinguishes exact, loose and wrong
pairs, one-to-many and many-to-many correspondences, and pairs that also link parts, such as a phrase to an image
region.

**Modalities go missing and compete.** <NoteLink to="missing-modalities" /> treats examples that lack a modality at
training or prediction time, modality dropout as a remedy, and imbalance between modalities that learn at different
speeds.

**Web-scale pairs are curated.** <NoteLink to="paired-data-curation" /> filters pairs whose halves disagree by a
similarity score, balances concepts, removes duplicates and test-set copies, and rewrites weak captions. A curated
subset can beat the whole pool <Cite id="gadre2023" />.

**Layout decides fusion.** <NoteLink to="multimodal-data-layouts" /> arranges inputs as one concatenated vector for
early fusion, separate model inputs for late fusion, interacting token sequences for intermediate fusion, or one
interleaved sequence for documents. <NoteLink to="modality-tokenisation" /> turns images, audio, video and series into
tokens by patching or vector quantisation.
