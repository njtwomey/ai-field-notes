<Definition>
**Error analysis** examines a model's mistakes to find their causes and rank the fixes. **Data debugging** traces
those causes to the training data, such as mislabelled, ambiguous, missing or misleading examples, and repairs the
data rather than the model. Together they close the loop from evaluation back to collection and labelling.
</Definition>

## What it is for

A validation score says how often a model is wrong, not why. The same error rate can come from
<NoteLink to="label-noise">label noise</NoteLink>, a missing subgroup, a shortcut feature or a model too small for the
task, and each calls for a different fix. Counting causes in a sample of errors shows which fix would remove the most
error, and so where engineering and labelling effort pays <Cite id="ng2018yearning" />. Tracking how each example
behaves during training finds the mislabelled and ambiguous ones without reading them all.

## Main ideas

**Sample, categorise, count.** <NoteLink to="error-analysis" /> samples misclassified examples, sorts them into a
taxonomy of causes and counts each cause. Ceiling analysis bounds the gain from fixing one cause or one pipeline stage.
The next fix is the one with the largest share of error and the lowest cost.

**Training dynamics reveal data problems.** <NoteLink to="training-dynamics-diagnostics" /> records each example's loss
and confidence across training. Dataset cartography sorts examples by mean confidence and its variability into easy,
ambiguous and hard regions <Cite id="swayamdipta2020" />. Examples that are learned and then forgotten, or never
learned, are often mislabelled.

**Hard examples carry the signal, and the noise.** <NoteLink to="hard-example-mining" /> concentrates training on
high-loss examples and on hard negatives in <NoteLink to="metric-learning">metric learning</NoteLink>. Among the hardest
examples are label errors and unannotated positives, which mining amplifies.

**Fix by collecting what is missing.** <NoteLink to="targeted-data-collection" /> gathers examples for slices where the
model fails and counterexamples that break shortcuts. Behavioural test suites such as CheckList specify the cases to
collect <Cite id="ribeiro2020checklist" />.
