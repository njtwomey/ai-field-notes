<Definition>
**Data**, in this Part, covers everything that happens to data before a model is fitted and around it once it is
deployed: what the data are and how they were produced, how they are examined, repaired, transformed, encoded and split,
how features are built and chosen, what changes for each kind and source of data, how labels and extra examples are
made, how privacy, bias and security are handled, how data are run in production, and which standard datasets and
environments the field uses.
</Definition>

## What it is for

A model learns what its data contain, including their mistakes. The sampling decides which population the model
describes. The labels decide what it predicts. The split decides whether its score means anything. The preprocessing
decides whether the same model can run in production. Each of these is a choice made before training, and each is a
common cause of models that look good offline and fail in use <Cite id="polyzotis2018" />. This Part treats those
choices as methods, with their own assumptions, failure modes and checks.

The Part owns data handling. It links to other Parts rather than repeating them where a method only consumes data:
<NoteLink to="spectral-features">spectral features</NoteLink> stay in Part II, <NoteLink to="class-imbalance">class
imbalance</NoteLink>, weak supervision and <NoteLink to="domain-adaptation">domain adaptation</NoteLink> in Part V,
tokenisation and <NoteLink to="image-filtering">image filtering</NoteLink> in Part VII, and drift detection,
<Gloss name="differential-privacy">differential privacy</Gloss> and monitoring of models in Part VIII.

## Main ideas

**Know where the data came from.** Measurement scales, the <NoteLink to="unit-of-observation">unit of
observation</NoteLink>, the sampling process and the way values were recorded fix what a dataset can support.
<NoteLink to="data-foundations" /> covers types and scales, data setups, selection bias, measurement error, collection
and documentation <Cite id="gebru2021" />.

**Look before fitting.** <NoteLink to="exploration-and-discovery" /> covers routine checks, profiles, plots and
projections, <NoteLink to="dependence-measures">dependence measures</NoteLink>, <NoteLink to="pattern-mining">pattern
mining</NoteLink>, <NoteLink to="causal-discovery">causal discovery</NoteLink> and <NoteLink to="dataset-search">dataset
search</NoteLink>.

**Every fitted step belongs to the model.** <NoteLink to="data-preparation" /> covers cleaning, imputation, scaling,
encoding, weighting, pipelines and splitting. Imputers, scalers and encoders are fitted on training rows only, and the
split mirrors how new data will arrive.

**Features carry knowledge.** <NoteLink to="feature-engineering-and-selection" /> covers constructed, temporal and
relational features, automated feature search, descriptors and embeddings, <NoteLink to="feature-stores">feature
stores</NoteLink>, and filter, wrapper and embedded selection <Cite id="kuhn2019" />.

**Data change after training.** <NoteLink to="shift-and-drift-in-data" /> separates covariate, label and concept shift,
shortcuts that fail under subgroup shift, and the trade-off in the length of the
<NoteLink to="training-window-and-freshness">training window</NoteLink>.

**Form and origin change the details.** <NoteLink to="data-by-modality" /> covers what differs for tables, series,
audio, images, video, text, documents, graphs, molecules, maps, <NoteLink to="three-dimensional-data">3D data</NoteLink>
and multimodal pairs. <NoteLink to="data-by-domain" /> covers the traps of twelve fields, from clinical records to
advertising logs.

**The data can be improved directly.** <NoteLink to="data-centric-learning" /> covers
<NoteLink to="labelling-and-annotation">labelling and annotation</NoteLink>,
<NoteLink to="augmentation-and-synthetic-data">augmentation and synthetic data</NoteLink>,
<NoteLink to="data-valuation">data valuation</NoteLink> and selection, and <NoteLink to="error-analysis">error
analysis</NoteLink>.

**Data carry risks and need operations.** <NoteLink to="privacy-bias-and-security" /> covers identifiability, bias and
poisoning. <NoteLink to="data-operations" /> covers formats, versioning, tests, monitoring and
<NoteLink to="training-serving-skew">training-serving skew</NoteLink>.

**Shared datasets and environments make results comparable.** <NoteLink to="standard-datasets" /> lists the standard
datasets of each area with their known problems. <NoteLink to="reinforcement-learning-environments" /> does the same for
simulators and offline logs.

## How the notes fit together

The Part has twelve subjects. They fall into five groups.

1. **Understanding the data:** <NoteLink to="data-foundations" />, then <NoteLink to="exploration-and-discovery" />.
2. **Turning data into model inputs:** <NoteLink to="data-preparation" />, then
   <NoteLink to="feature-engineering-and-selection" />, then <NoteLink to="shift-and-drift-in-data" />.
3. **Adapting to the kind of data:** <NoteLink to="data-by-modality" /> and <NoteLink to="data-by-domain" />.
4. **Improving and safeguarding data:** <NoteLink to="data-centric-learning" />,
   <NoteLink to="privacy-bias-and-security" /> and <NoteLink to="data-operations" />.
5. **Reference:** <NoteLink to="standard-datasets" /> and <NoteLink to="reinforcement-learning-environments" />.

Read the first two groups in order. Data foundations and data preparation are the core: most other notes in the Part
refer back to the data setups, leakage and pipeline notes. The modality and domain subjects cross the preparation
subject. Preparation holds the general technique, written for tables first, and each modality chapter adds only what
changes, linking back. The last three groups can be read in any order once the core is in place. The reference group is
for looking things up.

The Part assumes basic probability and statistics from Part I, such as <NoteLink to="conditional-probability" /> and

<NoteLink to="empirical-risk-minimisation" /> and <NoteLink to="cross-validation" />.

here. Network architectures in Part VI take the encoded and tokenised inputs.
<NoteLink to="deployment-and-monitoring">Deployment and monitoring</NoteLink> in Part VIII, such as
