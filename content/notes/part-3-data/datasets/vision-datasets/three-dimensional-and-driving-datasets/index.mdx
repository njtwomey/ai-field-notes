<Definition>
**3D and autonomous driving datasets** are benchmark sensory collections capturing spatial physical reality through
explicit 3D representations—including synthetic polygonal CAD meshes, calibrated RGB-D indoor depth reconstructions, and
mobile sensor rigs equipped with multi-beam LiDAR scanners, automotive radars, high-resolution cameras, and RTK GPS/IMU
navigation units <Cite id="chang2015shapenet, geiger2012, caesar2020nuscenes" />.
</Definition>

Unlike 2D images which suffer from projective perspective distortion and depth ambiguity, 3D and driving benchmarks
measure metric spatial geometry directly. They formalize 3D object bounding box estimation $(x, y, z, w, l, h, \theta)$,
point cloud semantic segmentation, multi-object tracking, and future trajectory forecasting in real-world coordinate
frames <Cite id="sun2020waymo" />.

---

## What These Datasets Measure

3D benchmarks formalize several distinct physical and robotic perception regimes:
1. **Synthetic CAD Mesh & Point Cloud Classification**: Reasoning over unordered point sets
   $\mathcal{P} \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ for object classification and part segmentation invariant to $SE(3)$ rigid
   transformations (ModelNet, ShapeNet) <Cite id="chang2015shapenet" />.
2. **Indoor RGB-D Scene Reconstruction**: Semantic voxelization and 3D bounding box detection inside cluttered indoor
   rooms from handheld depth cameras (ScanNet).
3. **Automotive 3D Object Detection**: Predicting 3D oriented bounding boxes for vehicles, pedestrians, and cyclists
   from LiDAR sweeps and panoramic camera rigs, evaluated by **3D Average Precision (3D AP)** and **nuScenes Detection
   Score (NDS)** <Cite id="caesar2020nuscenes" />.
4. **Multi-Sensor Fusion**: Evaluating late and early fusion architectures (e.g. BEVFormer, PointPillars) combining
   sparse metric LiDAR point clouds with dense semantic camera RGB pixels.

## Detailed Benchmark Profiles

### KITTI: Autonomous Driving Benchmark Pioneer (2012)

Introduced by Geiger, Lenz, and Urtasun <Cite id="geiger2012" />:
- **Sensor Setup**: A Volkswagen Passat equipped with two stereo color cameras, two grayscale cameras, a Velodyne
  HDL-64E rotating LiDAR, and an RTK GPS/IMU navigation unit.
- **Historical Standard**: Established the canonical benchmarks for monocular depth estimation, stereo disparity, visual
  odometry, optical flow, and 3D bounding box detection across three difficulty levels (*Easy*, *Moderate*, *Hard*)
  based on bounding box height, occlusion, and truncation.

### nuScenes: 360-Degree Multimodal Fusion (2020)

Developed by Motional (Caesar et al. <Cite id="caesar2020nuscenes" />):
- **Sensor Rig**: 6 synchronized cameras providing complete $360^\circ$ panoramic coverage, 1 roof-mounted 32-beam
  LiDAR, 5 automotive radars, and IMU/GPS.
- **nuScenes Detection Score (NDS)**: Replaced isolated AP with a composite metric combining mean Average Precision
  (mAP) with Average Translation Error (ATE), Average Scale Error (ASE), Average Orientation Error (AOE), Average
  Velocity Error (AVE), and Average Attribute Error (AAE):
  $$
  \text{NDS} = \frac{1}{10} \left[ 5 \cdot \text{mAP} + \sum_{\text{TP} \in \mathcal{E}} (1 - \min(1, \text{TP})) \right]
  $$
- **Bird’s-Eye-View (BEV) Evolution**: Catalyzed modern Bird’s-Eye-View (BEV) transformer architectures that map
  multi-camera perspective inputs into a unified metric ground-plane representation.

### Waymo Open Dataset (2020)

Released by Waymo (Sun et al. <Cite id="sun2020waymo" />):
- **Scale and Density**: Over 1,150 20-second segments collected across diverse operational design domains (San
  Francisco urban canyons, Phoenix suburban highways) under daytime, nighttime, rain, and dust conditions.
- **High-Resolution Sensors**: Top-mounted custom long-range LiDAR producing significantly higher point densities than
  32-beam commercial scanners, with over 12 million high-quality 3D bounding boxes.

### ShapeNet: Synthetic 3D Model Repository (2015)

Constructed by Chang et al. <Cite id="chang2015shapenet" />:
- **3D Manifold**: Over 51,300 clean 3D CAD models organized across 55 common object categories with WordNet mappings.
- **Pioneering 3D Deep Learning**: The standard corpus on which PointNet, PointNet++, DeepSDF, and NeRF 3D novel view
  synthesis were formulated.

## Choosing a 3D or Driving Dataset

- **Geometric Point Cloud & Mesh Analysis**:
  - ShapeNet / ModelNet (CAD models, part segmentation)
- **Indoor Robotic Navigation & RGB-D Mapping**:
  - ScanNet (RGB-D reconstructed indoor room environments)
- **Multimodal Autonomous Vehicle Perception (Cameras + Lidar + Radar)**:
  - *High density point clouds & large scale*: Waymo Open Dataset
  - *Complete 360-degree sensor fusion & HD maps*: nuScenes
- **Legacy Autonomous Vehicle Baseline Verification**:
  - KITTI (Standard benchmark for historical comparisons)

## Known Problems and Benchmark Pitfalls

1. **LiDAR Beam Sparsity at Range**: Point cloud density drops with the inverse square of distance ($1/r^2$). Distant
   objects ($> 50$ meters) may consist of only 3 to 5 discrete LiDAR points, making classification impossible from LiDAR
   alone without camera fusion.
2. **Geographic and Weather Bias**: Most driving datasets are collected in sunny conditions across select Western cities
   (California, Arizona). Adverse weather (heavy blizzard, torrential rain, fog) and developing-world chaotic traffic
   conditions are severely under-represented <Cite id="caesar2020nuscenes" />.
3. **Temporal Rolling Shutter and Motion Distortion**: High-speed vehicles cause rotational LiDAR distortion during the
   100ms sweep period, requiring precise ego-motion deskewing and IMU timestamp calibration.
