<Definition>
**Vision and multimodal datasets** are the image, video, 3D, image-text and robot datasets on which visual models are
trained and compared. Each note groups datasets by task, says what each measures and at what scale, and lists the known
problems with its labels, licences and coverage.
</Definition>

## What it is for

Progress in computer vision has been measured on a sequence of benchmarks, and models are still pretrained on them.
<Gloss name="imagenet" />, with over a million labelled images in a thousand classes, made large-scale supervised
training possible <Cite id="deng2009" />. <Gloss name="coco" /> set the protocol for detection and segmentation
<Cite id="lin2014" />. Web-scale image-text pools such as LAION-5B replaced curated labels with captions
<Cite id="schuhmann2022" />. Knowing a dataset's construction tells what a score on it means: which classes, which
cameras, which countries, how the labels were made, and what has since been found wrong.

## Main ideas

**Classification benchmarks grew in scale and grain.** <NoteLink to="image-classification-datasets" /> runs from
<Gloss name="mnist" /> and <Gloss name="cifar-10" /> to ImageNet, Places365 and the fine-grained species of iNaturalist.

**Localisation needs denser labels.** <NoteLink to="detection-and-segmentation-datasets" /> covers
<NoteLink to="image-annotation-formats">Pascal VOC</NoteLink>, COCO, Cityscapes, ADE20K, LVIS with its long tail of
categories, and the billion masks of SA-1B.

**People are a sensitive subject.** <NoteLink to="face-and-human-datasets" /> covers LFW, CelebA, FFHQ, MPII Human Pose
and Human3.6M, together with problems of consent, privacy and demographic imbalance.

**Time and space add dimensions.** <NoteLink to="video-datasets" /> covers UCF101, Kinetics, Something-Something, AVA
and Ego4D. <NoteLink to="three-dimensional-and-driving-datasets" /> covers ShapeNet, ScanNet and the driving suites
KITTI, nuScenes and Waymo Open, with lidar, radar and camera.

**Pairs and actions extend vision.** <NoteLink to="image-text-datasets" /> covers caption sets such as COCO Captions and
Flickr30k, web-scale pools such as LAION-5B and <NoteLink to="paired-data-curation">DataComp</NoteLink>, and
<Gloss name="qa">question answering</Gloss> in VQA v2 and Visual Genome. <NoteLink to="robot-learning-datasets" />
covers teleoperated trajectories in Open X-Embodiment, RT-1, DROID and BridgeData V2.

## How the notes fit together

Start with <NoteLink to="image-classification-datasets" />, which the other notes refer back to. Detection and
segmentation, faces and humans, and video each extend it in one direction. 3D and driving
data, image-text data and robot data are read last and in any order.
