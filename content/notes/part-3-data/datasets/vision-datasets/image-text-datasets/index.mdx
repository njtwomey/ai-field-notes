<Definition>
**Image-text and multimodal datasets** are benchmark corpora containing paired visual artifacts
$X_v \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times C}$ and natural language sequences $X_t \in \mathcal{V}^L$. Spanning
human-written descriptive captions, dense scene graphs, visual question-answering pairs, and multi-billion-scale raw web
crawls of HTML `<img>` tags and `alt`-text, they serve as the foundational pretraining fuel for Vision-Language Models
(VLMs), contrastive dual-encoders (CLIP), and text-to-image diffusion generators
<Cite id="radford2021, schuhmann2022" />.
</Definition>

Before web-scale multimodal pairing, computer vision models were constrained to fixed discrete label sets (e.g.
ImageNet's 1,000 classes). Multimodal datasets enabled open-vocabulary zero-shot generalization by projecting image
embeddings and text embeddings into a shared semantic latent space <Cite id="radford2021" />.

---

## What These Datasets Measure

Multimodal benchmarks evaluate distinct architectural paradigms and cross-modal reasoning capabilities:
1. **Cross-Modal Retrieval**: Matching images to text queries and vice versa, evaluated via Recall@K
   ($\text{R@1}, \text{R@5}, \text{R@10}$) under bidirectional InfoNCE contrastive losses <Cite id="radford2021" />.
2. **Visual Question Answering (VQA)**: Given an image and an open-ended natural language question, predicting the
   correct answer requiring spatial, logical, and perceptual reasoning <Cite id="goyal2017" />.
3. **Structured Visual Grounding & Scene Graphs**: Mapping images to grounded directed graphs $G = (V, E)$ where nodes
   represent localized objects and edges represent spatial, semantic, or action relationships (Visual Genome)
   <Cite id="krishna2017" />.
4. **Data Filtering and Quality Scaling**: Evaluating data curation algorithms (heuristic deduplication, CLIP-score
   thresholding, aesthetic filtering) on downstream zero-shot accuracy (DataComp).

## Detailed Benchmark Profiles

### MS COCO Captions (2014)

Augmented onto the MS COCO detection benchmark:
- **Crowdsourced Redundancy**: 5 distinct human annotators described each image independently without seeing others'
  submissions.
- **Linguistic Focus**: Annotators were instructed to describe visible facts and objects without inferring intentions or
  unobservable context, creating the gold standard evaluation set for caption evaluation metrics (BLEU-4, METEOR, CIDEr,
  SPICE).

### Conceptual Captions (CC3M / CC12M, 2018–2021)

Developed by Google Research (Sharma et al. <Cite id="sharma2018" />):
- **Automated Curation Pipeline**: Crawled billions of web pages, extracting images with `alt`-text attributes.
- **Rule-Based Filtering**: Applied strict text filters removing boilerplate SEO keywords, pornographic text, and
  low-frequency proper nouns, substituting specific entities with high-level hypernyms (e.g. replacing a specific
  celebrity name with *actor* or *politician*).
- **Bridge to Scale**: Demonstrated that large-scale weakly paired web data outperformed small human-annotated caption
  sets when pretraining deep dual-encoders.

### LAION-5B: Open Multi-Billion Scale Web Scrape (2022)

Released by the Large-scale Artificial Intelligence Open Network (Schuhmann et al. <Cite id="schuhmann2022" />):
- **Scale**: 5.85 billion image-text pairs parsed from Common Crawl dumps (2.32B English, 2.26B multilingual, 1B
  image-text with high aesthetic/similarity scores).
- **CLIP Filtering**: Each candidate URL was downloaded and passed through a pretrained OpenAI CLIP ViT-B/32; pairs with
  cosine similarity below $\tau = 0.28$ were discarded.
- **Democratization of Generative AI**: Provided the underlying open-weights training corpus for Stable Diffusion,
  OpenCLIP, and hundreds of academic foundation models.

### VQA v2: Mitigating Language Prior Shortcuts (2017)

Constructed by Goyal et al. <Cite id="goyal2017" /> to fix severe shortcut learning in VQA v1:
- **Language Bias Collapse**: In early VQA benchmarks, models could achieve $> 70\%$ accuracy on questions starting with
  *"What sport is..."* simply by predicting *"tennis"*, completely ignoring the input image.
- **Balanced Counter-Examples**: VQA v2 paired every question with two visually similar images that produce opposite
  answers (e.g. an image where the answer is *"yes"* and an image where the answer is *"no"* for the identical
  question), forcing models to ground reasoning in visual pixels.

## Choosing a Multimodal Dataset

- **Clean Evaluation & Benchmarking**:
  - *Dense captioning & retrieval*: MS COCO Captions / Flickr30k
  - *Visual QA with balanced counter-examples*: VQA v2
- **Structured Reasoning & Scene Graphs**:
  - Visual Genome (Dense attributes, object-object relationships)
- **Data-Centric Filtering Competitions**:
  - DataComp (Standardized filtering tracks from 12M to 1B scales)
- **Large-Scale Vision-Language Pretraining**:
  - *Curated web alt-text (3M–12M)*: Conceptual Captions
  - *Open-source web scale (> 1B)*: LAION-5B / Common Crawl

## Known Problems and Benchmark Pitfalls

1. **Severe Text-Image Mismatch**: Web `alt`-text frequently describes site navigation (e.g. *"download icon"*, *"click
   here to view photo"*), banner ads, or abstract emotions rather than physical image contents, introducing substantial
   open-set noise <Cite id="schuhmann2022" />.
2. **Harmful Content and Copyright Violations**: Unfiltered web scrapes ingest copyrighted artwork, personal photographs
   scraped without consent, toxic slurs, and CSAM, requiring rigorous safety filtering audits <Cite id="birhane2021" />.
3. **Language Prior Hallucination**: Multimodal LLMs trained on paired web data develop strong linguistic priors,
   frequently hallucinating common objects (e.g. asserting a dog is wearing a collar when it is bare) due to textual
   co-occurrence patterns in training text.
