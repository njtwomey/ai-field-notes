<Definition>
**Video datasets** are visual benchmark collections of ordered image frame sequences
$V \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times H \times W \times C}$ capturing temporal dynamics, physical causality, motion trajectories,
and human-object interactions. They formalize spatio-temporal representation learning, global action recognition,
temporal action localization, atomic spatio-temporal tube detection, and first-person egocentric perception
<Cite id="carreira2017, gu2018ava, grauman2022ego4d" />.
</Definition>

Adding the temporal dimension ($T$) dramatically increases computational complexity and introduces fundamental modeling
challenges: distinguishing true temporal causality from static background scene bias, modeling long-range temporal
dependencies, and handling camera ego-motion versus scene motion.

---

## What These Datasets Measure

Video benchmarks evaluate models along distinct spatio-temporal axes:
1. **Trimmed Action Classification**: Predicting a single action category for short pre-segmented video clips
   ($3\text{--}10$ seconds), evaluated by Top-1 and Top-5 accuracy (UCF101, Kinetics) <Cite id="carreira2017" />.
2. **Temporal Reasoning vs Scene Bias**: Distinguishing fine-grained physical transformations (e.g. *pushing something
   from left to right* vs *right to left*) where single static frames provide zero distinguishing signal
   (Something-Something v2).
3. **Spatio-Temporal Action Localization (Action Tubes)**: Detecting bounding boxes across contiguous video frames and
   classifying atomic human actions simultaneously, evaluated via Frame-mAP at $\text{IoU} \ge 0.5$ (AVA)
   <Cite id="gu2018ava" />.
4. **First-Person Egocentric Understanding**: Analyzing wearable camera video to predict episodic memory, hand-object
   manipulations, and future action forecasting from an embodied perspective (Ego4D) <Cite id="grauman2022ego4d" />.

## Detailed Benchmark Profiles

### Kinetics: The ImageNet of Video (2017–Present)

Created by DeepMind (Carreira and Zisserman <Cite id="carreira2017" />):
- **Scale and Evolution**: Began as Kinetics-400 (300k 10-second YouTube clips across 400 action classes) and expanded
  to Kinetics-600 and Kinetics-700.
- **Impact**: Established standard 3D convolutional architectures (I3D, SlowFast, X3D) and video transformers
  (TimeSformer, ViViT). Kinetics pretraining became the universal foundation for downstream video tasks, mirroring
  ImageNet's role in 2D vision.

### Something-Something v2: True Temporal Understanding (2017)

Constructed by TwentyBN (Goyal et al. 2017) to expose "shortcut learning" in action models:
- **The Shortcut Problem**: In Kinetics, a 2D model evaluating a single frame can often guess *playing tennis* simply by
  detecting a tennis court.
- **Compositional Actions**: Something-Something uses crowd workers performing basic physical manipulations using
  arbitrary everyday objects (e.g. *dropping something into something*, *pretending to open something*, *turning
  something upside down*). Because backgrounds and objects vary arbitrarily, models cannot rely on spatial context and
  must track motion vectors across frames.

### AVA: Atomic Visual Actions (2018)

Developed by Google Research (Gu et al. <Cite id="gu2018ava" />):
- **Dense Spatio-Temporal Ground Truth**: Annotates 80 atomic actions (e.g. *stand*, *talk to*, *listen to*, *carry*)
  across 15-minute feature film segments at 1-second intervals.
- **Multiple Concurrent Actions**: Each actor is localized by a bounding box and associated with multiple simultaneous
  atomic labels (e.g. a person can be simultaneously *sitting*, *drinking*, and *talking*).

### Ego4D: Massive Egocentric World Video (2022)

A consortium benchmark led by Meta AI and 13 academic universities across 9 countries <Cite id="grauman2022ego4d" />:
- **Unprecedented Scale**: 3,670 hours of daily-life video captured by 931 participants wearing head-mounted cameras
  across diverse global cultures.
- **Five Benchmark Challenges**: Episodic Memory (natural language queries localizing past visual events), Hands and
  Objects (fine-grained physical manipulation), Audio-Visual Diarization, Social Interactions (gaze, attention), and
  Forecasting (predicting immediate future actions and trajectories).

## Choosing a Video Dataset

- **General Video Backbone Pretraining**:
  - Kinetics-400 / Kinetics-700 (Large-scale YouTube actions)
- **Physical Reasoning & Motion Dynamics**:
  - Something-Something v2 (Object-agnostic physical manipulations)
- **Localized Action Tube Detection**:
  - AVA (Spatio-temporal actor bounding boxes in long video)
- **Embodied AI & Wearable First-Person Video**:
  - *Unscripted daily life & episodic memory*: Ego4D
  - *Fine-grained cooking & kitchen manipulations*: EPIC-Kitchens

## Known Problems and Benchmark Pitfalls

1. **Static Appearance Leakage**: In standard action recognition benchmarks, static background textures correlate
   heavily with labels, allowing 2D image classifiers without temporal modeling to achieve $> 70\%$ accuracy on video
   benchmarks.
2. **YouTube Link Rot**: Datasets that distribute YouTube video URLs rather than raw byte streams (such as Kinetics and
   ActivityNet) degrade rapidly as videos are deleted or set to private, causing reproducibility drift over time.
3. **Prohibitive Computational Overhead**: Training 3D convolutions or space-time attention transformers on raw video
   frames requires orders of magnitude more compute and memory bandwidth than 2D vision.
