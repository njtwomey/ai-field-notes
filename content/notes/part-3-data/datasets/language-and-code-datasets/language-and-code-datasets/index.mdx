<Definition>
**Language and code datasets** are the corpora and benchmarks of <Gloss name="nlp">natural-language processing</Gloss>
and code generation. Corpora supply the trillions of tokens used to pretrain <Gloss name="language-model">language
models</Gloss>. Benchmarks fix a set of tasks, inputs and reference answers on which models are compared. Each note
groups one family, says what it measures, and lists its known problems.
</Definition>

## What it is for

Language models are trained on web-scale corpora and judged on benchmarks, and both have changed quickly. Task suites
such as <Gloss name="glue" /> <Cite id="wang2018glue" /> were saturated within a few years of their release. Pretraining
corpora moved from filtered Common Crawl snapshots such as C4 <Cite id="raffel2020" /> to curated mixtures of trillions
of tokens. Knowledge and reasoning benchmarks such as <Gloss name="mmlu" /> <Cite id="hendrycks2021" /> took over as the
headline measures. Two problems recur throughout: benchmarks leak into training corpora, and models learn the shortcuts
of a benchmark's format.

## Main ideas

**Classic NLP benchmarks fix tasks.** <NoteLink to="nlp-benchmark-datasets" /> covers GLUE and SuperGLUE,
<Gloss name="squad" />, MultiNLI, CoNLL-2003 and WMT. Shortcuts in <Gloss name="nli">natural language inference</Gloss>
data and quick saturation limit what they show today.

**Pretraining corpora are filtered crawls.** <NoteLink to="language-model-corpora" /> covers Common Crawl, C4, The Pile,
RefinedWeb, Dolma and FineWeb, with their contamination, copyright and machine-generated text.

**Language-model benchmarks probe knowledge and skill.** <NoteLink to="language-model-benchmarks" /> covers MMLU, GSM8K,
GPQA and SWE-bench, and the contamination, answer-format artefacts and <NoteLink to="test-set-reuse">leaderboard
overfitting</NoteLink> that affect them.

**Code has its own corpora and tests.** <NoteLink to="code-datasets" /> covers The Stack and StarCoder data for
pretraining, and HumanEval, MBPP and APPS for program synthesis. Thin unit tests let wrong programs pass.

**Documents, conversations and languages.** <NoteLink to="document-understanding-datasets" /> covers FUNSD, DocVQA,
RVL-CDIP and PubLayNet. <NoteLink to="instruction-and-dialogue-datasets" /> covers FLAN, Alpaca, OpenAssistant,
LMSYS-Chat-1M and UltraFeedback, where length bias and imitation of a stronger model's style are common.
<NoteLink to="multilingual-datasets" /> covers FLORES, XTREME, OPUS and XNLI, where translated
<NoteLink to="test-sets">test sets</NoteLink> carry artefacts of translation.
