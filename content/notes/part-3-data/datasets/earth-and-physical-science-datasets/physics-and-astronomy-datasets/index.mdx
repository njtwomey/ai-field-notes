<Definition>
**Physics and astronomy datasets** are curated scientific collections of simulated dynamical systems, experimental
particle collision records, and astronomical sky survey observations used to train machine learning models for neural
operator PDE solving, high-energy particle identification, and cosmological property estimation.
</Definition>

## What these datasets measure

Physical and astronomical benchmarks evaluate machine learning models across continuum mechanics, subatomic particles,
and deep cosmological scales:

- **Continuum Mechanics & Neural PDE Operators:** Learning solution operators for time-dependent and boundary-value
  partial differential equations (PDEs)—including fluid dynamics (Navier-Stokes equations), wave propagation, Darcy flow
  in porous media, and magnetohydrodynamics. Models map initial conditions and boundary parameter fields to continuous
  trajectory solutions across arbitrary spatial resolutions.
- **Multiscale Physical Simulation:** Evaluating cross-domain physical surrogates across diverse regimes (turbulence,
  shock waves, astrophysical accretion disks, acoustic scattering) to replace computationally expensive numerical
  solvers.
- **High-Energy Particle Physics:** High-dimensional sensor measurements from collider detectors (tracking chambers,
  electromagnetic calorimeters, muon spectrometers) recording subatomic collision events. Primary tasks include jet
  tagging (distinguishing quark, gluon, top-quark, and Higgs jets), track reconstruction, and anomalous event discovery
  beyond the Standard Model.
- **Astronomical Sky Surveys & Galaxy Morphology:** Multiband optical and infrared telescope images and spectroscopic
  energy distributions. Tasks evaluate morphological galaxy classification (elliptical, spiral arms, mergers),
  photometric redshift ($z$) estimation, and transient event discovery (supernovae, gravitational lensing).

### PDEBench

Introduced by Takamoto et al. in 2022 (<Cite id="takamoto2022pdebench" />):
- **Systematic PDE Coverage:** Provides a standardized benchmark covering 11 classical partial differential equation
  families: 1D Burgers' equation, 1D/2D Advection, 1D/2D Diffusion-Reaction, 2D Darcy Flow through heterogeneous media,
  2D Shallow Water equations, and 1D/2D/3D Compressible Navier-Stokes equations under subsonic and supersonic turbulent
  conditions.
- **Operator Learning Focus:** Specifically designed to benchmark Neural Operators—such as Fourier Neural Operators
  (FNO) and DeepONet—which learn mappings between infinite-dimensional function spaces. Models are trained at lower
  spatial grid resolutions (e.g., $128 \times 128$) and evaluated zero-shot at higher unseen resolutions (e.g.,
  $512 \times 512$ or $1024 \times 1024$), testing true **mesh-invariance**.
- **Metrics:** Evaluates normalized root mean square error (nRMSE) over extended autoregressive rollout horizons to
  expose numerical drift and shock wave smearing.

### The Well

Created in 2024 by the Polymathic AI collaboration:
- **Multiphysics Breadth:** Encompasses 16 diverse physical simulation systems spanning acoustics, active matter,
  convective fluid dynamics, mantle convection, magnetohydrodynamics, and gravitational cosmological structure
  formation.
- **Foundation Benchmark:** Standardizes simulation outputs across dimensions, coordinate systems, and boundary
  conditions (periodic, Dirichlet, Neumann) to establish the first multi-task foundation models capable of generalizing
  physical dynamical priors across completely distinct physics domains.

### CERN Open Data Portal

Maintained by CERN for the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) collaborations:
- **Detector Telemetry:** Releases petabytes of proton-proton collision data from the CMS, ATLAS, ALICE, and LHCb
  detector experiments at center-of-mass energies up to $13\text{ TeV}$.
- **Machine Learning Benchmarks:** Popular subsets (such as the Top Quark Tagging Reference Dataset and the TrackML
  Particle Tracking Challenge) provide preprocessed particle point clouds and 4-momentum vectors $(p_x, p_y, p_z, E)$
  for collision constituents.
- **Jet Tagging Architectures:** Modern architectures treat particle collision jets as unordered geometric point clouds
  or graphs, using permutation-invariant models (ParticleNet, Particle Transformer) with Lorentz group equivariance.

### Galaxy Zoo

Initiated in 2007 by Chris Lintott and the Citizen Science Alliance (<Cite id="lintott2008galaxyzoo" />):
- **Crowdsourced Annotations:** Over 100,000 volunteer participants visually classified more than 900,000 galaxy images
  from the Sloan Digital Sky Survey.
- **Decision Tree Taxonomy:** Questions follow a branched morphology tree: Is the galaxy smooth or has features/disk? Is
  it edge-on or face-on? Does it exhibit spiral arms? How many spiral arms (1, 2, 3, 4, or more)? Is there a central
  stellar bar? Are there signs of gravitational tidal mergers?
- **Fractional Probabilities:** Because human classifications vary, labels are provided as continuous debiased agreement
  fractions, serving as a canonical benchmark for probabilistic computer vision under human labeling uncertainty.

### Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS)

The most comprehensive astronomical optical sky survey in history:
- **Instrumentation:** Collected using the dedicated 2.5-meter optical telescope at Apache Point Observatory in New
  Mexico, photographing more than one-third of the night sky across five optical filters ($u, g, r, i, z$).
- **Spectroscopic Catalog:** Encompasses detailed optical spectra for more than 3 million individual celestial objects.
- **Core ML Applications:** Photometric redshift estimation (predicting a galaxy's cosmological distance and lookback
  time purely from 5-band photometric colors without taking expensive spectra) and spectroscopic automated line fitting.

## Choosing a dataset

- **Solving partial differential equations & learning operators?**:
  - Standardized operator benchmark (Burgers, Navier-Stokes, Darcy) → PDEBench
  - Large-scale multiphysics foundation modeling (16 physics systems) → The Well
- **Subatomic particle physics and collider events?**:
  - CERN Open Data / Top Tagging (4-momentum vectors, point-cloud jet tagging)
- **Astrophysical surveys and galaxy classification?**:
  - Visual galaxy morphology & merger classification → Galaxy Zoo (SDSS images)
  - Photometric redshift regression & stellar spectra → Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS)

## Known problems

1. **Spectral Bias and High-Frequency Dissipation in Neural Operators:** Deep learning surrogate models frequently
   suffer from **spectral bias**: neural operators prioritize learning smooth, low-frequency macroscopic fields while
   failing to resolve sharp gradients, discontinuous shock fronts, and turbulent high-frequency eddies.
2. **Resolution and Mesh Invariance Claims:** Many neural operator architectures claim mesh-invariance, but their
   generalization error deteriorates significantly when tested on out-of-distribution grid scales or non-uniform
   adaptive meshes (AMR) unseen during training.
3. **Sim-to-Real Discrepancies in Particle Physics:** Machine learning models trained on Monte Carlo event generators
   (such as Pythia or Geant4) easily overfit to subtle modeling choices in the simulation's hadronization tuning,
   causing performance drops when applied to raw experimental collision runs.
4. **Extreme Signal-to-Background Ratios in Collider Events:** New physics discovery (e.g., rare Higgs decays or dark
   matter candidates) involves detecting handfuls of signal events buried under billions of Standard Model background
   collisions, requiring false-positive suppression exceeding six orders of magnitude.
