<Definition>
**Chemistry and materials datasets** are curated collections of small molecules, macromolecules, crystalline solids, and
catalytic surfaces—represented as 2D molecular graphs, 3D Cartesian coordinates, or periodic crystal unit
cells—annotated with quantum-chemical properties computed via density functional theory (DFT) or measured experimentally
in bioassays.
</Definition>

## What these datasets measure

Chemical and materials benchmarks evaluate machine learning models across several physical representation domains:

- **Quantum Chemical Molecular Properties:** Predicting electronic, geometric, and energetic properties of small organic
  molecules (e.g., highest occupied molecular orbital [HOMO], lowest unoccupied molecular orbital [LUMO], HOMO-LUMO gap,
  dipole moment, isotropic polarizability, zero-point vibrational energy) computed via high-level quantum mechanical
  methods (DFT).
- **Physical Chemistry & Bioactivity:** Predicting macroscopic solubility, membrane permeability (blood-brain barrier
  penetration), target binding affinities, and toxicological responses from molecular topology.
- **Inorganic Crystal Properties:** Predicting formation energies per atom, bandgaps, elastic moduli, and thermodynamic
  phase stability of periodic crystal lattices defined by fractional atomic coordinates and lattice vectors
  $(\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}, \mathbf{c})$.
- **Catalytic Surface Interactions:** Modeling the interaction energy and atomic forces between small molecular
  adsorbates ($CO, NO_x, OH$) and heterogeneous electrocatalyst surfaces during chemical reactions.
- **SE(3) Equivariance & Invariance:** Testing whether geometric graph neural networks preserve rotational and
  translational symmetries in 3D Euclidean space.

### QM9

Introduced by Ramakrishnan et al. in 2014 (<Cite id="ramakrishnan2014qm9" />):
- **Chemical Space:** Covers 133,885 neutral organic molecules consisting of up to nine heavy atoms ($C, N, O, F$) and
  saturated with hydrogen.
- **Computational Consistency:** Every structure was geometry-optimized and analyzed using Density Functional Theory at
  the B3LYP/6-31G(2df,p) level.
- **Target Quantities:** Provides 19 electronic, thermodynamic, and energetic properties: dipole moment ($\mu$),
  isotropic polarizability ($\alpha$), orbital energies ($\epsilon_{\text{HOMO}}, \epsilon_{\text{LUMO}}$), energy gap
  ($\Delta\epsilon$), electronic spatial extent ($\langle R^2 \rangle$), zero-point vibrational energy (ZPVE), heat
  capacity ($C_v$), and internal energies ($U_0, U, H, G$).
- **Benchmark Role:** The universal testbed for SE(3)-equivariant and invariant 3D graph neural networks (e.g., SchNet,
  DimeNet, EGNN), evaluated via Mean Absolute Error (MAE) relative to chemical accuracy thresholds (e.g.,
  $1\text{ kcal/mol} \approx 0.043\text{ eV}$).

### ZINC

Curated by the Irwin and Shoichet Laboratories at UCSF:
- **Commercial Availability:** A massive digital database of commercially available chemical compounds prepared
  specifically for computational virtual screening and molecular docking.
- **Structural Subsets:** Ranges from clean drug-like subsets (hundreds of thousands of compounds with molecular weight
  $\le 500$, octanol-water partition coefficient $\log P \le 5$) to multi-billion compound combinatorial libraries.
- **Generative Benchmarks:** A standardized 250,000-molecule subset from ZINC serves as the primary benchmark for
  molecular generative models (VAEs, normalizing flows, diffusion models) evaluated on validity, novelty, and synthetic
  accessibility (SAS).

### MoleculeNet

Introduced by Wu et al. in 2018 as a comprehensive benchmark suite for molecular machine learning:
- **Suite Taxonomy:**
  - *Quantum Mechanics:* QM7, QM8, QM9.
  - *Physical Chemistry:* ESOL (aqueous water solubility), FreeSolv (hydration free energy), Lipophilicity.
  - *Biophysics:* BACE ($\beta$-secretase 1 inhibition), HIV (viral replication inhibition), PCBA (high-throughput
    screening assays).
  - *Physiology & Toxicity:* Tox21 (12 toxicity pathway assays), ClinTox (FDA clinical approval and trial toxicity
    failure), BBBP (blood-brain barrier penetration).
- **Evaluation Standards:** MoleculeNet established the necessity of **scaffold splitting** over random splitting to
  assess out-of-distribution generalization across distinct core chemical chemotypes.

### Materials Project

Co-founded by Kristin Persson and Gerbrand Ceder at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory:
- **Periodic Solids:** Houses over 154,000 inorganic materials with computed structural, thermodynamic, and electronic
  properties.
- **Crystalline Representations:** Crystal lattices are represented via unit cell parameters and fractional atomic
  coordinates with 3D periodic boundary conditions.
- **Targets:** Formation energy per atom ($\text{eV/atom}$), bandgap ($\text{eV}$), space group symmetry, Fermi energy,
  and convex hull thermodynamic stability ($\Delta E_{\text{hull}}$).

### Open Catalyst 2020 (OC20)

Joint initiative by Meta AI (FAIR) and Carnegie Mellon University (<Cite id="zitnick2020ocp" />):
- **Catalytic Challenge:** Accelerating the discovery of low-cost electrocatalysts for renewable energy conversion
  (carbon capture, hydrogen fuel production, nitrogen reduction).
- **Data Scale:** Over 1.2 million DFT structural relaxations encompassing more than 130 million single-point DFT
  calculations of small adsorbates interacting with multi-element catalyst surfaces.
- **Core Tasks:**
  - *Structure to Energy and Forces (S2EF):* Predicting atomic forces ($F_i = -\nabla_{R_i} E$) and total potential
    energy given an atomic configuration.
  - *Initial Structure to Relaxed Structure (IS2RS):* Predicting the final equilibrium 3D atomic coordinates directly.
  - *Initial Structure to Relaxed Energy (IS2RE):* Predicting the relaxed adsorption energy from the unrelaxed starting
    state.

## Choosing a dataset

- **Predicting quantum-mechanical properties of small molecules?**:
  - QM9 (134k organic molecules in 3D, 19 DFT electronic & thermodynamic targets)
- **Evaluating 2D molecular property prediction or bioassays?**:
  - MoleculeNet (17 diverse benchmarks; ESOL, BACE, Tox21, scaffold splits)
- **Evaluating molecular generative models (de novo drug design)?**:
  - ZINC (ZINC250k subset; SMILES representations, drug-like filters)
- **Predicting solid-state periodic crystal properties?**:
  - Materials Project (154k inorganic crystals, DFT formation energies, bandgaps)
- **Modeling surface catalysis and atomic force fields?**:
  - Open Catalyst 2020 (OC20, 1.2M DFT relaxations, energy & force prediction)

## Known problems

1. **Computational Approximation Discrepancy (DFT vs. Experiment):** Quantum mechanical properties in QM9 and OC20 are
   computed via DFT approximations (such as generalized gradient approximations like PBE or B3LYP). DFT carries known
   systematic errors (e.g., self-interaction error, underestimation of bandgaps), meaning models trained on DFT outputs
   learn to emulate the approximation rather than true physical reality.
2. **Scaffold Collapse in Random Splitting:** Because chemical libraries contain multiple structural analogues generated
   around a single core chemotype, random splitting leads to inflated test accuracy. Strict Murcko scaffold or
   cluster-based splitting (<NoteLink to="molecular-representations">molecular representations</NoteLink>) is required
   to reflect real medicinal chemistry screening.
3. **SE(3) Equivariance Violations:** Standard Cartesian coordinates change under rotations and translations. If a model
   lacks equivariant message-passing architectures, rotating a molecule in space produces unphysical changes in
   predicted scalar energies and vector forces.
4. **Adsorbate Binding Multimodality:** In catalyst datasets like OC20, an adsorbate molecule can settle into multiple
   local energy minima depending on slight perturbations in its starting pose, creating high variance in structural
   relaxation trajectories.
