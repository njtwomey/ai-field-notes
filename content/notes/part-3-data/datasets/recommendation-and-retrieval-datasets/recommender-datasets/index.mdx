<Definition>
**Recommender datasets** are curated collections of user-item interactions—comprising explicit ratings, implicit
engagement signals (clicks, watch time, purchases), exposure logs, and contextual features—used to train and benchmark
collaborative filtering, sequential session modeling, and click-through rate (CTR) prediction systems.
</Definition>

## What these datasets measure

Recommendation benchmarks evaluate algorithms across diverse interaction modalities and feedback loops:

- **Explicit Feedback:** Direct numerical ratings (e.g., 1-to-5 stars) reflecting user sentiment. Primary tasks focus on
  rating prediction, root-mean-square error (RMSE) minimization, and matrix factorization.
- **Implicit Feedback:** Binary or count-based interaction events (clicks, page views, stream completions, bookmarks)
  where negative instances are unobserved rather than explicitly disliked. Evaluated using top-$K$ ranking metrics: Hit
  Rate@$K$, Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain (NDCG@$K$), and Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR).
- **Sequential & Session-Based Dynamics:** Chronologically ordered interaction sequences testing next-item prediction
  (e.g., SASRec, BERT4Rec) without long-term user profile IDs.
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR) & Conversion Prediction:** Sparse high-dimensional tabular logs combining user
  demographics, contextual signals, and ad creative features, evaluated via binary log-loss and Area Under the ROC Curve
  (AUC).
- **Exposure Bias & Fully Observed Ground Truth:** Benchmarks logging impression logs or complete interaction matrices
  to evaluate inverse propensity scoring (IPS) and counterfactual debiasing.

### MovieLens

Curated by GroupLens Research at the University of Minnesota (<Cite id="harper2015movielens" />):
- **Generations and Tiers:** Available in canonical releases spanning MovieLens 100K, 1M, 10M, 20M, 25M, and 32M.
- **Data Attributes:** Clean user-item-rating-timestamp tuples paired with film title, release year, genre tags, and
  genome tag relevance scores computed from user-applied keywords.
- **Benchmark Role:** The universal baseline dataset for collaborative filtering algorithms—from classical User-User KNN
  and Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) to Neural Collaborative Filtering (<Cite id="he2017ncf" />) and Mult-VAE.

### Netflix Prize

Released in 2006 to inaugurate the famous 1-million-USD competition:
- **Historical Significance:** Challenged the machine learning community to beat Netflix's production Cinematch
  algorithm's RMSE ($0.9525$) by $10\%$. Achieved in 2009 by the BellKor's Pragmatic Chaos ensemble ($0.8567$ RMSE).
- **Key Contributions:** Catalyzed the development of modern latent factor models, temporal dynamics factorization
  (TimeSVD++), and restricted Boltzmann machines for collaborative filtering.

### Amazon Reviews

Compiled by Julian McAuley's laboratory at UC San Diego:
- **Product Categories:** Covers 29 distinct Amazon product domains (Electronics, Books, Clothing, Movies & TV, Home,
  Video Games).
- **Rich Context:** Each record contains star ratings, timestamped verified purchase flags, product co-purchasing graphs
  ("also bought", "also viewed"), item brand/category hierarchies, and full natural language review text.
- **Sequential Modeling:** Serves as the standard benchmark for sequential recommendation algorithms (e.g., SASRec,
  TiSASRec), where models must predict the next product a customer purchases given their historical trajectory.

### Yelp Open Dataset

Maintained for academic research by Yelp:
- **Multimodal Attributes:** Contains 6.9 million customer reviews and ratings alongside rich geographic coordinates,
  operating hours, check-in timestamps, photo features, and social friendship networks among reviewers.
- **Evaluation Settings:** Ideal for geo-aware, context-aware, and social recommendation systems.

### Last.fm

Derived from the Last.fm music streaming platform:
- **Implicit Feedback Focus:** Captures continuous user listening logs ("scrobbles") without explicit negative ratings.
  Play counts reflect implicit preference strength, requiring models to implement confidence-weighted alternating least
  squares (iALS) or Bayesian Personalized Ranking (BPR).

### Criteo Display Advertising Dataset

The premier industry benchmark for click-through rate (CTR) prediction:
- **Feature Composition:** 45 million consecutive display ad impressions comprising 13 continuous integer features
  (counts, delays) and 26 high-cardinality categorical features hashed into 32-bit integers.
- **Architectural Benchmark:** Standard testbed for deep learning recommender models designed for dense embedding
  lookups and feature cross interactions: Factorization Machines (FM), DeepFM, DLRM, and AutoInt.

### MIND (Microsoft News Dataset)

Released by Microsoft Research in 2020:
- **Impression Sessions:** Contains user click logs from Microsoft News, preserving the complete impression session: the
  list of displayed candidate news headlines alongside binary user click labels.
- **Content-Rich Recommender:** News articles feature rich textual titles, abstracts, bodies, and entity linkings,
  evaluating the fusion of large language models with user browsing histories.

### KuaiRec

Released in 2022 by Kuaishou:
- **Dense Matrix Ground Truth:** Solves the fundamental limitation of traditional implicit feedback datasets where
  unobserved items are ambiguous. KuaiRec collected a fully observed interaction submatrix where 1,411 users were forced
  to evaluate 3,327 micro-videos, providing an unbiased ground truth testbed to evaluate counterfactual debiasing and
  exposure models.

## Choosing a dataset

- **Evaluating explicit collaborative filtering (star ratings / RMSE)?**:
  - Small, standardized collaborative filtering baseline → MovieLens 1M / 20M
  - Historical latent factor & time dynamics benchmark → Netflix Prize
- **Evaluating implicit top-K ranking or sequential recommendation?**:
  - Next-item purchase prediction from sequences → Amazon Reviews (Books, Electronics)
  - User impression click logs & content-aware news → MIND (Microsoft News)
  - Implicit streaming play counts & scrobbles → Last.fm
- **Evaluating large-scale ad click-through rate (CTR) prediction?**:
  - Criteo Display Advertising (45M impressions, high-cardinality hashing)
- **Evaluating counterfactual exposure debiasing & causal recommendations?**:
  - KuaiRec (dense user-item interaction matrix, unobserved debiasing)

## Known problems

1. **Selection Bias and Missing Not at Random (MNAR):** Users do not rate random items; they selectively choose and rate
   items they expect to enjoy or strongly dislike (<NoteLink to="interaction-logs">interaction logs</NoteLink>). Unrated
   items in datasets like MovieLens cannot be treated as negative feedback.
2. **Popularity Bias and Power-Law Item Distributions:** A tiny fraction of blockbuster movies or viral products account
   for the vast majority of user interactions. Models optimized purely on accuracy gravitate toward recommending
   ubiquitous popular items, achieving high NDCG while failing to deliver personalized discovery.
3. **Temporal Leakage in Random Splits:** Evaluating sequential recommenders using uniform random item splits creates
   severe forward-looking leakage. Evaluation must strictly employ **leave-one-last** or temporal cutoff splitting,
   training only on events occurring before timestamp $t$.
4. **Position and Exposure Bias in Click Logs:** In ad and news datasets (Criteo, MIND), items placed in top screen
   positions receive disproportionate clicks regardless of relevance, confounding user intent with user interface
   layout.
