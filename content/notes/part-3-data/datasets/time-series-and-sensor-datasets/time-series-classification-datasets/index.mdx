<Definition>
**Time-series classification datasets** are standardized benchmark archives pairing discrete temporal sequence vectors
or multi-channel matrices $\mathbf{X} \in \mathbb{R}^{D \times T}$ with categorical class labels
$Y \in \{1, \dots, K\}$. Anchored by the curated UCR and UEA repositories, they formalize algorithmic evaluation across
1-Nearest-Neighbor Dynamic Time Warping (1-NN DTW), shapelet transforms, bag-of-patterns dictionaries, ensemble methods
(HIVE-COTE), and deep convolutional networks (InceptionTime) <Cite id="dau2019, bagnall2017, bagnall2018uea" />.
</Definition>

Temporal sequence classification presents unique challenges distinct from static tabular or image classification:
temporal ordering enforces autocorrelation, phase shifts and amplitude scaling obscure structural similarity, and
sequence lengths vary <Cite id="bagnall2017" />.

---

## What These Datasets Measure

Time-series classification benchmarks evaluate distinct algorithmic representations:
1. **Elastic Distance Distortions**: Measuring invariance to non-linear temporal warping along the time axis using
   **Dynamic Time Warping (DTW)** with tuned warping window constraints $w$:
   $$
   d_{\text{DTW}}(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y}) = \min_{\pi} \sum_{(i, j) \in \pi} (x_i - y_j)^2
   $$
2. **Phase-Independent Shapelets**: Extracting discriminative, phase-invariant local subsequence motifs (shapelets)
   whose presence or absence dictates class membership.
3. **Frequency & Dictionary Representations**: Discretizing continuous trajectories via Symbolic Aggregate ApproxiMation
   (SAX) and Bag-of-SFA-Symbols (BOSS) to evaluate word-frequency histograms.
4. **Multivariate Inter-Channel Dependencies**: In UEA datasets, capturing synchronized cross-channel covariances across
   triaxial accelerometers or multi-lead ECG sensors <Cite id="bagnall2018uea" />.

## Detailed Benchmark Profiles

### The UCR Time Series Archive (2002–2018)

Curated by Eamonn Keogh and colleagues at UC Riverside, expanded to 128 datasets in 2018 <Cite id="dau2019" />:
- **Historical Gold Standard**: The definitive benchmark that unified the time-series data mining community, preventing
  cherry-picked evaluations on bespoke private datasets.
- **Data Categories**:
  - *Image Outlines*: 2D object silhouettes transformed into 1D radius vectors (e.g. *ArrowHead*, *BeetleFly*,
    *Herring*).
  - *Sensor & Smart Meter Readings*: Telemetry, acoustic sensors, smart grid power consumption (e.g. *FordA*,
    *ElectricDevices*).
  - *Motion & Physiological Signals*: Electrocardiograms, motion tracking, photoplethysmography (e.g. *ECG200*,
    *GunPoint*).
- **Standard Protocol**: Predefined, immutable train and test splits to ensure exact reproducibility across competing
  publications.

### The UEA Multivariate Archive (2018)

Introduced by Bagnall et al. <Cite id="bagnall2018uea" /> from the University of East Anglia:
- **Multidimensional Sensor Streams**: Expanded benchmark evaluation from 1D signals to multi-channel sensor arrays
  ($D \in [2, 1345]$ channels), accommodating modern Internet of Things (IoT) hardware.
- **Core Findings**: Revealed that simple dimensional flattening fails on high-dimensional streams; models must
  explicitly balance intra-channel temporal features against inter-channel cross-correlations.

## Choosing a Time-Series Classification Dataset

- **Univariate Algorithmic Bake-Off & Baseline Verification**:
  - *Canonical 128-dataset bake-off*: UCR 2018 Archive
  - *Fast sanity checks*: GunPoint (N=200) / ECG200
- **Multi-Channel IoT & Wearable Sensor Fusion**:
  - *Motion, robotics, & biometrics*: UEA Multivariate Archive
  - *Multi-lead aviation gestures*: NATOPS
- **Large-Scale Deep Learning Scaling**:
  - *High sample volume*: ElectricDevices (N=16k) / FordA (N=5k)

## Known Problems and Benchmark Pitfalls

1. **Default Split Overfitting**: Because the original UCR archive published fixed train/test partitions, dozens of
   algorithms were implicitly tuned to test set quirks. Modern bake-off protocols enforce 30 random resample folds to
   verify stability <Cite id="bagnall2017" />.
2. **Synthetic Image Outline Proliferation**: Nearly a third of UCR datasets are 1D pseudo-time series generated by
   unwrapping 2D image contours (such as leaves or fish silhouettes). While useful for rotation invariance testing, they
   lack the physical noise, drift, and non-stationarity of true temporal sensor feeds <Cite id="dau2019" />.
3. **Class Imbalance Masking**: Accuracy is the traditional reported metric on UCR/UEA, which can conceal poor
   minority-class recall on heavily skewed medical datasets.
