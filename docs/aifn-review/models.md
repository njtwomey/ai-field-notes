### Reviewed and found correct (no finding)

These were checked against their references, line by line where it mattered:

- **MCMC.** Metropolis–Hastings and the independence sampler (NaN and ±∞ handling). HMC, and multinomial NUTS against
  Stan's `base_nuts.hpp` (both extra U-turn checks, in both directions; biased progressive sampling; divergence; the
  `accept_stat` sum). Slice NUTS against Hoffman and Gelman, Algorithm 3. Dual averaging (Algorithm 5 and 6 updates;
  ε̄ frozen after warmup).
- **MCMC diagnostics.** ESS (Geyer's initial positive and monotone sequences) and split R̂, against ArviZ `_ess` and
  `_rhat` term by term.
- **EP and ADF.** Cavity, site update with damping, power EP, and the log-evidence identity.
- **Belief propagation.** Tree schedules on forests are exact (probe p3: marginals and log Z exact). Flooding is
  two-phase. Evidence is handled.
- **BOCPD.** The log-space recursion, hazard edge cases (H = 0 and H = 1) and pruning renormalisation.
- **Kalman filter.** Joseph form and the log-likelihood from one LU of S.
- **ODEs.** Dormand–Prince (tableau, error norm, FSAL, scipy step control, Hairer's starting step). `odeAdjoint`
  (signs of a′ and g′, checkpoints). TR-BDF2 start-up coefficients. Hermite event location.
- **SDEs.** Milstein and Platen's derivative-free scheme.
- **Systems.** `ss2tf`, `tf2ss`, `tf2zpk` for both domains; `cont2discrete` Tustin; margins (probe p5: GM 2 at √3
  rad/s and PM 27.1° for 4/(s + 1)³; GM ∞ and PM 51.8° for 1/(s(s + 1))).
- **Transport.** Sinkhorn in log domain, `wasserstein1d`, `monotonePlan`, the Hungarian path.
- **Attention.** Masks with caches and rolling windows; RoPE (half and interleaved layouts; YaRN ramp and magnitude
  against Hugging Face); ALiBi slopes for non-powers of two; T5 buckets against Hugging Face; KV-cache trimming
  (keeps exactly the keys the window needs); multi-head latent attention.
- **Decoding.** Logit processors (Hugging Face order), beam search, speculative sampling (residual and bonus).
- **SSMs.** HiPPO-LegS against S4; linear and matrix recurrences with an initial state.
- **Layers.** BatchNorm running statistics (unbiased running variance, as torch); inverted dropout.
- **Losses and metrics.** Probe p1 covered AUROC ties and multiclass, AP ties, MCC, κ, ARI, NMI, Kendall τ-b and log
  loss. NaN is returned where scikit-learn substitutes 0 or 1 (MCC with a constant prediction, R² with constant y).
  That follows the documented policy that "an undefined ratio is reported, not hidden".
- **Text.** Case folding (the Cherokee exceptions are right), the GPT-2 pattern, byte-level symbols, offsets (UTF-16
  units throughout).
- **Interpreter.** Seeding is deterministic and `seed()` re-keys the root. Error lines are mapped for runtime errors.

### Test quality

- Signal, systems, nn, losses, metrics and text are tested against scipy, torch, scikit-learn or Hugging Face fixtures
  (`test/helpers` `F`, `fixture(...)`). The **inference** family (stochastic, EP, message passing, exact, variational,
  filtering) and **dynamics/fields**, **dynamics/sde** and the **ODE adjoint** use internal consistency and closed
  forms only, with no reference fixture. Candidates: NUTS or HMC draws are not comparable across implementations, but
  `effectiveSampleSize` and `splitRhat` on fixed arrays against ArviZ, `kalmanFilter` against filterpy or statsmodels,
  and `beliefPropagation` on a loopy graph against pgmpy are.
- Before this review, no test differentiated a loss or activation at a boundary or at a large argument (the BCE and
  ELU bugs). A generated "gradient finite at the domain edges" sweep over the registered losses and activations would
  catch this class of bug.
- No test covered inputs past the JS spread limit. The two new tests now cover the fixed sites.

**Core code that names a model or dataset.** I scanned every export of the nine families. Only `STOP_WORDS` (a
dataset, table above) clearly breaks C1. Borderline cases that I recommend keeping:

- `SelectiveSsm` (Mamba's SSM layer) and `BahdanauAttention`/`LuongAttention` are named layers in the sense of torch's
  `nn.LSTM`: reusable components, not models.
- `brownianMotion`, `ornsteinUhlenbeck` and `geometricBrownianMotion` (`dynamics/sde/processes.ts`) are named processes.
  They are analogous to named distributions, which are core.
- The BOCPD conjugate segment models are families, not models.

The named chain models already moved out (architecture, "Decided 2026-09-30").
