# aifn core review: `foundation/`

Area 1 of the core review (2026-10-01): `aifn-js/core/src/foundation/**` and `aifn-js/core/test/foundation/**`. Rules
and format: `.scratch/aifn/progress/review-common.md`. Log: `.scratch/aifn/progress/review-foundation.md`.

## Scope and method

The review read every interpreter (`tensor/trace.ts`, `autodiff/{reverse,forward,batch,transforms,custom,graph,check}`),
the primitive machinery (`tensor/primitive.ts`, `registry.ts`) and the primitives of `tensor/` (elementwise,
reductions, structure, gather, products, complex, scan), the fourier and filter rules, `trace/` (runners, protocol,
differentiate), `random/` (stream, philox, samplers, replicate), `space/`, `pytree/` and `registry/`. The generated
primitive suite (`test/primitives.test.ts`) checks every rule at random interior points. The probes therefore targeted
what it cannot see: zeros, ties, −∞, rank-0 tensors against numbers, number examples under `vmap`, complex tangents
through custom rules, repeated arguments, nested and mixed transforms, and the runners' checkpoint and snapshot
semantics. Every finding below was reproduced by a scratch probe (`.scratch/tmp/review-foundation/probe*.test.ts`)
before it was reported or fixed.

What held up: perturbation confusion (`d/dx [x · d/dy (x + y)]` = 1), closures over outer tracers, `grad∘grad`,
`jvp∘grad`, `vmap∘grad`, `grad∘vmap`, nested `vmap`, `inAxes`/`outAxes` (negative too), forward and reverse Jacobians
for every number/vector combination, `checkpoint` to second order, `customVjp`/`customJvp` under every pairing of
transforms (real values), `stopGradient` across levels, the scan recursion for n = 0…5, slicing geometry with negative
steps and empty axes, the Philox key encoding (injective, prefix-free), word accounting of the samplers, and the
linear-filter vjp/jvp derivations (checked by hand against y = A⁻¹Bx).

State after the fixes: `vitest` core 144 files / 7440 passed, applications 94 / 727 passed, `tsc -p
tsconfig.aifn.json` clean, prettier and oxlint clean on the touched files.

## Notes for the owner

- The fixed rules change values only at the edge points named (pow at a = b = 0 and at a = 0 in b; `sum` of a rank-0
  tensor now returns a rank-0 cotangent). Core and applications suites are unchanged otherwise.
- `seek` now throws on a stream that disagrees with the trace's key. If the lab ever wants "trace key, ignore the
  stream", drop the stream there instead.
- Nothing in `refinement.md`'s open core items overlaps these findings.
