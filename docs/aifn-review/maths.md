# aifn core review, area 2: numerics, probability, optim, graph

Scope: `aifn-js/core/src/{numerics,probability,optim,graph}/**` and their tests (about 37k lines). Reviewer log:
`.scratch/aifn/progress/review-maths.md`. Rules and format: `.scratch/aifn/progress/review-common.md`. Items already
open in `.scratch/aifn/refinement.md` are cited, not re-reported.

## Method

The existing fixtures already compare the distributions, quadrature, roots, programming and information modules with
scipy at their interior points, and the generated primitive suite checks every derivative rule at random interior
points. The probes therefore went to the edges, by differential testing against reference libraries
(scratch files in `.scratch/tmp/maths/`):

- **Special functions** (`special.test.ts` + `cmp_special.py`): about 1900 points against mpmath at 400 digits:
  tails, large and tiny arguments, near poles and zeros, tiny shapes (1e-3) and large ones (1e5), and every inverse
  by round trip.
- **Hypothesis tests** (`tests.test.ts` + `cmp_tests.py`): 197 results against scipy 1.18 and statsmodels: every
  alternative, ties, zeros, n = 3, exact and asymptotic paths, intervals, multiple testing, Ljung–Box.
- **Samplers** (`samplers.test.ts`, `vm.test.ts`): KS against each family's own cdf and moment z-scores at 44 hard
  parameter sets (Gamma(0.05), Beta(0.05, 0.05), Student t(0.5), von Mises κ from 1e-6 to 1e5, truncated normals at
  −30 and +8, Binomial(1e6, 1e-5), Poisson(1e4), Hypergeometric(1000, 999, 500)).
- **Linear algebra** (`lin.test.ts`, `svd.test.ts`): gradients against central differences at degenerate inputs
  (repeated eigenvalues and singular values, rank-deficient, wide and tall, singular, defective for expm).
- **Quadrature** (`quad.test.ts` + `cmp_rules.py`): Gauss–Legendre, –Hermite and generalised –Laguerre rules up to
  n = 180 against numpy and scipy; `integrate` on divergent, singular and infinite-range integrands.
- **Graphs** (`gen_graphs.py` + `graph.test.ts`): 55 random multigraphs (self-loops, parallel edges, negative DAGs)
  against networkx: Dijkstra, Bellman–Ford, Floyd–Warshall, Kruskal and Prim, components, SCCs, `isDag`, max flow.
- **By reading**: Brent's method line by line against scipy's `brentq`; strong Wolfe against Nocedal and Wright
  Algorithms 3.5 and 3.6; the first-order rules against PyTorch and optax; every KL closed form; the bijector
  log-Jacobians; the eigh, SVD and det rules (Giles 2008, Townsend 2016); PCHIP and Akima slopes against scipy.

What held up: the special functions everywhere else on the grid (relative error at most 1e-12, mostly 1e-15), the
gamma and beta inverses down to 1e-300, every distribution sampler, every hypothesis test where the conventions agree
(t, z, binomial, Fisher, χ², G, ANOVA, one-sample KS, Shapiro–Wilk, Clopper–Pearson, Wilson, multiple testing,
Ljung–Box), the eigh, QR, det, logDet, Cholesky and expm gradients, the degeneracy errors of eigh and QR, all Gauss
rules, the `converged` flags of `integrate`, and all graph algorithms.

State after the fixes: vitest `test/numerics test/probability test/optim test/graph` 52 files, 1364 passed (5
skipped); `test/primitives.test.ts` and `root.test.ts` 2322 passed; `tsc -p tsconfig.aifn.json` clean; prettier clean
on the touched files.
