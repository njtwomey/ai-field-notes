## Method

- Every new core module was read line by line against its primary source: Hedge, fixed share and weighted majority
  (Cesa-Bianchi and Lugosi), OGD, FTRL and FTRL-Proximal (McMahan 2013), ONS (Hazan et al. 2007), IPS, SNIPS, DR and
  switch-DR (Wang et al. 2017), the slate pseudo-inverse estimator (Swaminathan et al. 2017), temperature, beta and
  Dirichlet calibration, Platt in the Lin–Lin–Weng form, split conformal, CQR, APS and RAPS, Markov chain classification,
  first-step analysis and the relaxation-time bounds (Levin, Peres and Wilmer), k-d and ball trees, LSH families, IVF,
  PQ and OPQ (Procrustes step), HNSW (Malkov and Yashunin, Algorithms 1, 2 and 4), NN-descent, harmonic label
  propagation and label spreading, GCN, GAT, GATv2 and GraphSAGE, MASS, STOMP and SCRIMP++, DTW with LB_Keogh and
  LB_Kim, PAA/SAX/MINDIST, the Grimshaw GPD profile likelihood, FastMCD, uPU and nnPU, the proportion and complementary
  losses, NPMI and UMass coherence, Dawid–Skene EM, confident learning and the anomaly detectors.
- Edge cases were probed by hand-built cases that became regression tests: floating-point ranks, chains where the target
  blocks the way to a lost state, tiny leaks out of a class, zero rows under the cosine distance, unlabelled graph
  components, saturated logits, bin boundaries, k > n.
- Duplicates were searched across both packages (softmax, logsumexp, ranks, z-scores, quantiles, k-means steps,
  nearest-codeword assignment, top-k).
- Lab: `make lab-shots` in both themes for every C, D and E1 page, after Train for the trained showcases (GNN,
  recommenders, feedback loop, topic models, MLP), and for a sample of B's migrated pages (nn/training, MDN, MoE, L-BFGS,
  neural ODE, decision trees, boosting, grokking). Every screenshot was read; the fixed pages were shot again.

What held up: the online learners' updates and bounds, every off-policy estimator (switch-DR reduces to the paper's
form), the calibration maps, APS/RAPS scores, the Markov fundamental-matrix formulas and mixing bounds, the trees'
pruning bounds (including the ball tree under squared Euclidean), OPQ's Procrustes step, HNSW's heuristic, the GNN
layers and edge softmax, STOMP's diagonal recurrence, DTW and both lower bounds, the GPD profile likelihood, FastMCD,
the PU risks, Dawid–Skene and confident learning.

State after the fixes: targeted suites green (core: conformal, markov, off-policy, neighbours, propagation, weak
losses, calibration, online, extremes; applications: anomaly, clustering, weak supervision). Full core suite 6501
passed, 1 failing suite (`foundation/registry/conformance`: E2's coupling bijector). Full applications suite 1032
passed, 1 failing (`factorAnalysis` model fixture: F2). Lab: `tsc -p tsconfig.lab.json` clean except E2's
`gym/agents/policy/offline.ts` and `learning/transfer/meta.ts`; `make lab-check` 297/0; oxlint clean on the touched
files (four pre-existing warnings); Prettier clean on the touched files (run from the repo root). Left to F2's final
green-up: `aifn-js/README.md` tables and `catalog.json` are stale from the agents still adding modules, and the name lint
flags E2's `encode`/`decode` against `foundation/space`.

triangulation, torch's min–max observers and half-to-even rounding, the STE mask, and GPTQ's column loop against the
reference code.
